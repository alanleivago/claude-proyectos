# Cae a través de la Tierra — brief

**Spec:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 62 s, 120 BPM. Music + box captions, Spanish. Style `pixdoc` (360×640).
**Format:** F5 fixed hero (the ball centred while the layers stream past), a HUD and a ghost (textbook model). Adapted from the "Drop a Ball Through Earth" reference; added: the garden and the girl, the Kola scale, the ghost racing beside the ball, the liquid-iron core, 0 g at the centre, the antipodes, the return to the garden.
**Physics:** PREM-like g(r) (linear to 10.68 m/s² at the core-mantle boundary, easing to 9.82 at the surface) integrated numerically, crossing scaled to 38.2 min; ghost = uniform density, simple harmonic, 42.2 min. Peak speed of the model ≈ 9.8 km/s ("casi 10 km/s").

## Claims and sources
- 38 min (real Earth, PREM) vs 42 min (uniform) — [Klotz, Am. J. Phys. 83, 231 (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.1342), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/50312-how-long-to-fall-through-earth.html)
- Gravity peaks at ~10.7 m/s² at the core-mantle boundary — [PREM value 10.6823 (ASPECT)](https://aspect.geodynamics.org/doc/doxygen/namespaceaspect_1_1constants_1_1earth_1_1prem__gravity.html); boundary depth 2,891 km — [MIT OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-570-structure-and-dynamics-of-the-cmb-region-spring-2004/f2869399d67fce18038231c9ecdaaa3a_lec1_1.pdf)
- Inner core boundary 5,150 km depth, radius 6,371 km (standard PREM values).
- Kola Superdeep Borehole: 12,262 m — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kola_Superdeep_Borehole)
- Core about as hot as the Sun's surface (~6,000 °C) — [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/the-center-of-the-earth-is-as-hot-as-the-sun-43631207)
- Spain ↔ New Zealand, Argentina ↔ China; most antipodes are ocean (≈15 % of land has land antipodes) — [Antipodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipodes)
- "76 min ida y vuelta" = 2 × 38.2 min in the same no-friction model.
