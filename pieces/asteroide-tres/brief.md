# asteroide-tres — What if an asteroid blew apart above your town? (variant B, English)

Same English texts as pieces/asteroide; everything else moved or changed so it differs from the Spanish version (pieces/meteorito).

## What is different from asteroide / meteorito
- **Three asteroids** instead of one: three new stars appear staggered (4.5 / 6.3 / 8.1 s), three fireballs come down from the upper LEFT with three streaks, three silent airbursts at 31.0 / 31.6 / 32.3 s (three flashes, three rings, three plumes of decreasing size).
- **Triple impact**: after the 0.7 s silence, three shockwaves at 57.3 / 58.5 / 59.7 s — each with its own shake, debris flying past the lens, a brown-out pulse, a boom and a new crack in the lens (three crack stars).
- **Layout mirrored and reseeded**: the sun on the RIGHT; the river nearer (−480 m) bending the other way; the bridge on the left, the church on the right; the railway nearer and the steam train running right → left; a new town, trees, taiga, clouds, cows, haystacks (new seeds); more red and copper in the autumn crowns.
- **A different porch**: blue-grey painted posts and rail, a white sawtooth valance with drilled suns.
- Sound: the English bed with the train and whistle panned to the right; three ringing tones for the three silent flashes; three booms and three glass cracks.
