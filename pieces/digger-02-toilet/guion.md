# Digger 02: The toilet (~235 words, ~85-90 s)

Operator… I dug up another human artifact. Humans called it… a toilet.

If you dig a hole in the snow and sit over it… you have a toilet.

But the hole fills up… so humans built a little wooden shed over a deep pit. This is an outhouse.

At night, nobody wanted to walk outside in the cold… so they kept a pot under the bed. This is a chamber pot.

And in the morning… they threw it out the window. Into the street. Where people were walking.

Gross.

So a man named John Harington built a seat with a water tank above it. Pull the handle… and the water flushes everything away. This is a flush toilet.

But the pipe went straight down to the sewer… and the sewer breathed back up. The whole house smelled.

Operator Jake asks: "Why doesn't my bathroom smell like a sewer?" Jake… look behind your toilet.

In 1775, a watchmaker named Alexander Cumming bent the pipe into an S.

Water gets stuck in the bend… and blocks the gas. This is a trap.

That's why there's always water in your toilet… it's a tiny wall between you and the sewer.

Humans spent almost two hundred years… just to stop their pipes from breathing on them?

Artifact understood. Logging off.
