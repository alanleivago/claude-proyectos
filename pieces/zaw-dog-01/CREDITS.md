## Images
- Textures, props, faces and the pictures on the walls: generated with Google Flow (Nano Banana) for this piece.
- Revision 2 (Flow, one image each): the lab scientist's face (`ref/flow/gen/*studio-passport*`) and the four funny dog pictures on the walls (`ref/flow/gen/*2x2-grid*`: beagle "Mona Lisa", pug school photo, corgi "Napoleon", retriever in a car window).
- Kitchen-window night sky: drawn in code (`prep_v2.py`) over the Flow street photo.
- Revision 3 (Flow, one image per call): the three people sheets (front / profile / back, A-pose) that the 3D humans are built from (`photohuman.py`), two product sheets (pillows, throw, mug, vase, dog bed, radiator, dog crate, basket, books, magazines, shoes, boots, ball, rope toy, flasks, treat jar, clipboard) and one texture sheet (kitchen wall, lab wall, roof shingles, siding).
- Sky photo: reused from pieces/lps-erfurt (ref/tex/sky_day.jpg).

- The dog: the approved zaw-dog v2 model (`pieces/zaw-dog-v2`), built from the original dog's real photos (side photo + the five face photos, plus a Flow sad face of the same dog). The rear and top Flow views made in revision 3 (`ref/flow/gen/2026-11-06T11-45-55*`, `*11-46-31*`) were superseded by v2 and are no longer in the picture.
- Kitchen picture: the family photo (Flow, from the first version) replaces the repeated pug picture.
