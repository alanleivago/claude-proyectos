# Only Way Out: A GTA 6 Story (comic script)

There is no voice-over. Every word on screen is lettered.

Key:
- `CAPTION` is a narrator box.
- `BUBBLE (who, type)` is a speech balloon. The types are normal, shout (spiky edge, bold), whisper (dashed edge, small), phone (jagged edge with a lightning tail), radio (jagged, monospace) and thought (cloud).
- `SFX` is lettered sound in the art.
- `[PANELS: ...]` is a comic-panel cover sequence and `[PARALLAX: ...]` is a layered parallax shot.
- Each beat holds 2 to 3 lettered items at most. Hold each beat at about 1 s per 2.5 words, with a 2 s minimum.

## 0. Opening (0:00-0:30)

**0.1** [PANELS: a GTA VI style cover splits into panels that reshuffle. Jason, Lucia]
- CAPTION: A fan story built from the trailers and the official cast.
- CAPTION: None of it is confirmed. This is our guess.

**0.2** [PANELS: title panel, Lucia's face half in shadow]
- CAPTION: Rockstar says Lucia Caminos got out of prison by sheer luck.
- CAPTION: We don't buy it.

## 1. The cell (0:30-1:40)

**1.1** [PARALLAX: Leonida Penitentiary cell at night, bars in the foreground, a strip light flickering. Lucia on the bunk]
- SFX: bzzzt... bzzzt
- CAPTION: Leonida Penitentiary. Lucia learned to fight before she learned to read.

**1.2** [PARALLAX: same cell, Lucia's fists, a scar on her knuckles]
- CAPTION: Fighting for her family put her in here. Nobody came to get her out.

**1.3** [PARALLAX: the door swings open and Méndez enters, STF on his vest]
- SFX: KA-CHUNK
- CAPTION: Then one night, a visitor. Méndez, Special Task Force.

**1.4** [PARALLAX: Méndez sits on the other bunk and slides a sheet across]
- BUBBLE (Méndez, normal): You've got a friend in the Keys. Jason Duval.
- BUBBLE (Méndez, normal): Ex-Army. Works for people we'd like to meet.

**1.5** [PARALLAX: close on Lucia, not looking at the paper]
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): I don't have friends.
- BUBBLE (Méndez, whisper): Then this should be easy.

**1.5b** [PARALLAX: Méndez leaning in, the strip light flickering over his face]
- BUBBLE (Méndez, normal): Eleven years left on your sentence. Or six weeks, and a phone.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, thought): Mamá's still waiting in that apartment.

**1.6** [PANELS: close-up of the pen and the signature, Lucia's knuckles white]
- SFX: scrrritch
- CAPTION: She signs. In the official version, that's the luck.

## 3. Release (2:50-3:55)

**3.1** [PARALLAX: prison gate in hard sun, Lucia walks out with a black band on her ankle]
- SFX: BZZZZ-CLANK
- CAPTION: Six weeks later. Same clothes she came in with, plus a monitor on her ankle.

**3.2** [PARALLAX: Jason leaning on a muscle car, sunglasses on]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): Told you I'd be here.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): You told me a lot of things.

**3.2b** [PARALLAX: Jason opening the passenger door for her]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): You look good, Lu.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): I look like a year of bad coffee.

**3.3** [PARALLAX: the Overseas Highway at sunset, green water, the car crossing the bridge]
- SFX: VRRRMMMMM
- CAPTION: Twenty miles south, and neither of them says a word.

**3.3b** [PARALLAX: inside the car, wind in Lucia's hair, Jason's hand on the gearstick]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): I kept your room. Well, your side of the bed.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): Smooth. Did the Army teach you that?

**3.4** [PARALLAX: gas station in Marathon, Jason glances at her ankle]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): What's that?
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): Parole. Curfew at ten, check-ins every Friday.

**3.5** [PANELS: flashback strip. Teenage Jason running from a store, Jason in uniform, Jason on a night boat]
- CAPTION: He believes her. With Lucia, Jason always has, even when he shouldn't.

**3.6** [PARALLAX: Jason's trailer at night, Jason asleep, Lucia on the step outside with the burner glowing]
- BUBBLE (Méndez, phone): First check-in, Caminos. What's he doing?
- BUBBLE (Lucia, whisper): Sleeping. Like a normal person.

## 4. The debt (3:55-5:05)

**4.1** [PARALLAX: Brian's dock at dusk, two beers in Brian's hands]
- CAPTION: Brian only brings two cold beers when it's bad news.
- BUBBLE (Brian, normal): Last run came up forty grand short. Coast Guard took the boat.

**4.2** [PARALLAX: same dock, closer on both]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): That wasn't on me.
- BUBBLE (Brian, normal): Everything on my water is on you, son. Thirty days.

**4.2b** [PARALLAX: Brian cracking a beer, not looking at Jason]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): Thirty days for forty grand?
- BUBBLE (Brian, normal): Twenty-nine if you keep talking.

**4.3** [PARALLAX: Lori comes down the steps with a plate of shrimp, smiling. Brian isn't]
- BUBBLE (Lori, normal): Bring that girl of yours by for dinner. Brian wants to meet her.

**4.4** [PARALLAX: Cal's porch at night, a police scanner glowing]
- SFX: kssshhh...
- BUBBLE (radio, radio): ...unit four, vessel in position...
- BUBBLE (Cal, whisper): Coast Guard knew exactly where that boat would be. Somebody's talking.

**4.5** [PARALLAX: Jason laughing it off. Cal writes a date on a wall covered in dates]
- CAPTION: Jason laughs. Cal adds the date to the wall.

**4.6** [PARALLAX: the porch, beer cans, Cal tapping the wall of dates]
- BUBBLE (Cal, normal): Every bust lands right after somebody new shows up. Every single one.
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): You said that about the mailman, Cal.

## 5. The job (5:05-6:35)

**5.1** [PARALLAX: Vice City at night, then the Jack of Hearts sign]
- SFX: BOOM-TSS BOOM-TSS
- CAPTION: Forty grand doesn't come from honest work. Jason asks around.

**5.2** [PARALLAX: back booth, Boobie under a painting of himself, Raul in linen beside him]
- BUBBLE (Boobie, normal): This is Raul. Raul likes people who don't panic.

**5.2b** [PARALLAX: Boobie leaning forward, rings catching the neon]
- BUBBLE (Boobie, normal): Brian's boy. I heard you owe the shark.
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): I heard you're hiring.

**5.3** [PARALLAX: Raul looking at Lucia]
- BUBBLE (Raul, normal): And you? You panic?
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): Not anymore.

**5.4** [PARALLAX: stage, Real Dimez filming a video while Dre'Quan holds the phone]
- BUBBLE (Dre'Quan, shout): Lower! Get the sign in the shot!
- CAPTION: Nobody watches the booth. That's what the booth is for.

**5.5** [PANELS: the plan. Armored-car route map, Raul's finger on a timetable, cash bag]
- CAPTION: Friday morning. One cash pickup. Half a million in a single bag.
- BUBBLE (Boobie, whisper): The label washes it the same week.

**5.6** [PANELS: Raul smiling]
- BUBBLE (Raul, normal): Easy score.

**5.6b** [PANELS: Jason and Lucia clink glasses, Real Dimez dancing behind them]
- BUBBLE (Jason, whisper): One job. We pay Brian, then we disappear.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, whisper): Where to?
- CAPTION: He doesn't have an answer. He never has.

**5.7** [PANELS: club bathroom, Lucia typing on a burner]
- CAPTION (as a phone screen): Friday. Morning. Raul Bautista.
- SFX: bip

**5.7b** [PANELS: Lucia's face in the bathroom mirror, neon bleeding through the door]
- BUBBLE (Lucia, thought): Only smart moves. That's what I promised.

## 6. It goes wrong (6:35-7:50)

**6.1** [PARALLAX: bank curb on a bright morning, the bag going into the car]
- CAPTION: Friday. The pickup goes clean for ninety seconds.

**6.1b** [PARALLAX: in the back seat, Lucia glancing at the burner in her lap]
- BUBBLE (Lucia, thought): Not yet. Please, not with him in the car.

**6.2** [PARALLAX: same street, police lights flooding in from every side]
- SFX: WEE-OOO WEE-OOO
- BUBBLE (Raul, shout): Too early! They're too early!

**6.3** [PANELS: chase. Tires, a shattered windshield, a helicopter]
- SFX: SCREEEECH / BLAM BLAM BLAM / KSSSH
- BUBBLE (Jason, shout): Take the wheel!

**6.3b** [PANELS: Jason hanging out the window, firing at a helicopter he can't hit]
- SFX: BRAKKA-BRAKKA
- BUBBLE (Raul, shout): Thirty years I've done this! Never this fast!

**6.4** [PARALLAX: the causeway at speed, palms streaking, a cruiser waiting at every turn]
- CAPTION: Every turn, a cruiser already waiting. As if someone gave them the route.

**6.5** [PARALLAX: red light in Little Haiti, Raul rolls out with the bag]
- SFX: THUD
- CAPTION: They lose the cops. They lose Raul too, and the bag goes with him.

**6.6** [PARALLAX: parking garage, the burner buzzing in Lucia's pocket]
- SFX: bzzt bzzt
- BUBBLE (Méndez, phone): That wasn't the deal. I wanted Duval in the car with the money.
- BUBBLE (Méndez, phone): Next time you hand me Jason, or you go back.

**6.7** [PARALLAX: Jason counting crumpled bills]

**6.8** [PARALLAX: close on Lucia]
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): Parole office.
- CAPTION: For the first time, the lie costs her something.

**6.8b** [PARALLAX: Jason frowning, still holding the bills]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): Parole calls in the middle of a getaway?
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): They call whenever they want.

## 7. Cal finds out (7:50-9:00)

**7.1** [PARALLAX: roadside motel in the rain, VACANCY neon, a knock on the door]
- SFX: KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK
- CAPTION: 2 a.m. Cal, soaked, holding a cassette like a confession.

**7.1b** [PARALLAX: doorway, Cal pushing past Jason into the room]
- BUBBLE (Cal, normal): Don't look at me like I'm crazy. Just listen to the tape.

**7.2** [PARALLAX: inside, Cal pressing play on a recorder]
- SFX: klik
- BUBBLE (radio, radio): ...confirm C.I. Caminos... C.I. Caminos...
- BUBBLE (Cal, normal): Three times, Jase. They said it three times.

**7.3** [PANELS: the truth sequence in red. The signature, the bathroom text, the garage call]
- CAPTION: Jason doesn't shout. That's worse.

**7.4** [PARALLAX: Jason on the edge of the bed, staring at the ankle monitor]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): How long?
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): Since before the gate. It was that or eleven more years.

**7.5** [PARALLAX: same room, the two facing each other]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): You could've told me.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, shout): And you'd have run! You've been running since you were fifteen!

**7.6** [PARALLAX: Jason's eyes, with ghost images of the boat and Cal's wall]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): The boat?
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): That wasn't me. I swear it wasn't me.

**7.7** [PARALLAX: Lucia alone in frame]
- CAPTION: She has no way to prove it.

**7.7b** [PARALLAX: Jason turns to the window, rain on the glass]
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): Everybody I ever trusted had a price, Lu.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): Mine was getting back to you.

**7.8** [PARALLAX: Cal in the doorway, headlights sweeping across the curtains]
- BUBBLE (Cal, whisper): If she's got a handler, the handler knows this room.
- SFX: VRRRM... click

## 8. Only way out (9:00-9:50)

**8.1** [PARALLAX: Grassrivers swamp at dawn, the truck stuck where the road becomes water, mist]
- CAPTION: Grassrivers. Then the road runs out.
- SFX: WHUP WHUP WHUP

**8.2** [PARALLAX: mangroves lit red and blue, Lucia's phone ringing]
- BUBBLE (Méndez, phone): Walk him to the road and you're free. Tonight. No monitor.

**8.2b** [PARALLAX: Lucia, phone at her ear, Jason ten steps ahead not looking back]
- BUBBLE (Lucia, phone): And if I don't?
- BUBBLE (Méndez, phone): Then you're both mine by sunrise.

**8.3** [PARALLAX: Jason, bleeding from the arm, holding a branch back for her]
- CAPTION: Two ways out. A road with Méndez at the end, or wherever Jason goes.

**8.3b** [PARALLAX: Jason turning back toward her, helicopter light sweeping the water]
- BUBBLE (Jason, shout): Lu! Come on, they're closing in!

**8.4** [PANELS: Lucia cracks the phone and it drops into the black water]
- SFX: KRAK / plunk

**8.5** [PANELS: a knife under the plastic band on her ankle]
- SFX: SNIP
- BUBBLE (Jason, normal): Thought you only made smart moves now.
- BUBBLE (Lucia, normal): This is the smartest one I've got.

**8.5b** [PANELS: the cut band sinking into the water beside the phone]
- CAPTION: The monitor sinks next to the phone.

**8.6** [PANELS: final cover. Jason and Lucia back to back, police lights on three sides, title ONLY WAY OUT]
- BUBBLE (Jason, whisper): Together?
- BUBBLE (Lucia, whisper): Together.

**8.7** [PANELS: pull back from the cover, sirens ringing the swamp]
- SFX: WEE-OOO
- CAPTION: We leave them here, surrounded. Maybe they walk away. Maybe they don't.

## 9. Close (9:50-10:05)

**9.1** [PANELS: the eight cards again, Lucia's second face now revealed]
- CAPTION: If you were Lucia, would you have kept the deal?
