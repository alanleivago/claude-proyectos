# Only Way Out: shot list v2 (cinematic)

Built from the approved script `comic_script.md` (74 beats, scenes 0–9) after the user's review of v1 ("muy flojo").

## Rules this list follows
- **Film language first.** Establishing shots, shot/reverse-shot, over-the-shoulder, inserts, reaction shots, cuts on action, camera moves (dolly, push-in, pan, crane, parallax). Comic devices (a panel split, SFX lettering) are rare accents, marked `ACCENT`.
- **A new pose and a new shot every time a character speaks.** Silent reaction shots also get their own pose.
- **Staging.** Every figure has a real support (bunk, chair, car seat, floor, rail) that exists in the plate. Each shot's plate is painted **from the same photo the pose comes from** (Flow edit: remove the person, keep the furniture and the person's cast shadow, repaint as a GTA VI background). Perspective, horizon, scale, contact shadow and light direction therefore match by construction. Foreground occluders (bars, bunk edge, car door) are listed per shot.
- **Text.** Dialogue = subtitle with the speaker's name (default). Speech bubbles only for key moments (`BUBBLE`). Narration captions = lower-left caption block. Thought = italic subtitle. SFX lettering = accent only.
- 12 fps stepped motion, GTA VI palette, saturation ×1.25. Average 3–4 s per shot.

Shot sizes: EWS extreme wide, WS wide, MWS medium wide, MS medium, MCU medium close-up, CU close-up, ECU extreme close-up, OTS over the shoulder, INS insert, POV. Angles: eye, low, high, top, dutch.
