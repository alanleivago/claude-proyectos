# Only Way Out: A GTA 6 Story

Narration only. The narrator voices everything; character lines are short quotes he reads or that appear in the speech balloons. Tags: `[PANELS: ...]` = comic-panel cover sequence, `[PARALLAX: ...]` = layered parallax scene.

## 0. Hook (0:00-0:40)

[PANELS: Jason, Lucia. A GTA VI cover splits into panels that reshuffle out of order]

You've got six days before GTA 6 tells us how it really ends. Here's the story we think Rockstar is hiding, and why Jason never sees it coming.

Rockstar's own words say Lucia Caminos got out of the Leonida Penitentiary by sheer luck. We don't buy it. So this is our version: a fan story built from the trailers and the official cast. None of it is confirmed, and next week we find out how wrong we were.

## 1. The cell (0:40-1:50)

[PARALLAX: Leonida Penitentiary cell at night, bars in the foreground, a strip light buzzing. Lucia, Méndez]

It starts where the first trailer started, in a cell with a buzzing strip light and a woman who has been counting the cracks in the ceiling for far too long.

Lucia learned to fight before she learned to read. Her father taught her. Fighting for her family is what put her in here, and nobody came to get her out.

Then one night the door opens and a man in a vest that says STF sits down on the other bunk. Méndez. Special Task Force.

He doesn't threaten her. He slides a sheet of paper across the blanket.

"You've got a friend in the Keys," Méndez says. "Jason Duval. Army. Works for people we'd like to meet."

Lucia doesn't look at the paper. "I don't have friends."

"Then this should be easy."

[PANELS: close-up panel of the signature, the pen, Lucia's knuckles]

She signs. In the official version, that's the luck.

## 2. Panel credits (1:50-2:50)

[PANELS: eight character cards, one per panel, each with name plate. Jason, Lucia, Cal, Brian, Raul, Boobie, Dre'Quan, Real Dimez]

Before we go further, meet the people who will decide how this ends.

Jason Duval. Grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the Army to get away from them, and came back to the Keys to work for the same kind of people. All he wants is an easy life.

Cal Hampton, Jason's best friend. He drinks beer on the dock and listens to Coast Guard radio all night, because he's sure somebody is lying to him. In this story he's right.

Brian Heder. He looks like a beach bum and moves like a great white shark. He's been running product through the Keys since the glory days, and Jason owes him.

Raul Bautista, a bank robber with charm and patience who is always looking for new talent.

Boobie Ike, who owns half of Vice City's nightlife. Real estate, a recording studio, and a strip club called the Jack of Hearts.

Dre'Quan Priest, Boobie's partner at Only Raw Records. More of a hustler than a gangster.

And Real Dimez, Bae-Luxe and Roxy, two friends from high school with one viral hit and a lot of followers watching.

Lucia is the last card, and her panel is the only one with a second face hidden behind it.

## 3. Release (2:50-4:00)

[PARALLAX: prison gate in hard sun, then the Overseas Highway through the Keys at sunset. Lucia, Jason]

Six weeks later the gate opens. Lucia walks out in the clothes she came in with and a black plastic band locked around her ankle.

Jason is leaning on a muscle car he can't afford, sunglasses on, like he's been there since sunrise. He probably has.

"Told you I'd be here," he says.

"You told me a lot of things."

They drive south with the windows down. The bridge runs out over green water, and for about twenty miles neither of them says a word, because there isn't a sentence big enough.

At a gas station in Marathon he notices the ankle monitor. She tells him it's parole: curfew at ten, check-ins every Friday.

He believes her. When it comes to Lucia, Jason has always believed her, even back when he shouldn't have.

[PANELS: quick flashback strip. Teenage Jason running from a store, Jason in uniform, Jason on a boat at night]

## 4. The debt (4:00-5:10)

[PARALLAX: Brian's dock and boat in the Keys at dusk, Lori in the background. Jason, Brian, Cal]

Brian Heder is waiting on the dock with two cold beers, which is how you know it's bad news.

"Last run came up short," Brian says. "Forty grand short. Coast Guard took the boat, and the boat was yours to look after."

"That wasn't on me."

"Everything on my water is on you, son."

Brian gives him thirty days. He doesn't say what happens on day thirty-one, and he doesn't have to.

Lori comes down the steps with a plate of shrimp and smiles at Jason like nothing was said. "You bring that girl of yours by for dinner," she says. "Brian wants to meet her." Brian doesn't smile at all.

Later, on Cal's porch, the scanner hisses in the dark. Cal turns it down and says something Jason will remember much too late.

"Coast Guard knew exactly where that boat would be. Exactly. Somebody's talking, man."

Jason laughs it off. Cal writes the date on the wall next to all the others.

## 5. The job (5:10-6:50)

[PARALLAX: Jack of Hearts club, Vice City, neon and smoke, dancers in silhouette. Boobie, Dre'Quan, Real Dimez, Raul, Jason, Lucia]

Forty thousand dollars doesn't come from honest work, so Jason does what he always does. He asks around.

The answer leads to Vice City, to a booth in the back of the Jack of Hearts. Boobie Ike sits under a painting of himself, and next to him is a gray-haired man in a linen shirt.

"This is Raul," Boobie says. "Raul likes people who don't panic."

Raul looks at Lucia first, the way a jeweler looks at a stone. "And you? You panic?"

"Not anymore."

On the stage, Bae-Luxe and Roxy are filming a video for Only Raw Records while Dre'Quan points a phone at them and yells about angles. Nobody in the club is looking at the booth, and that's why the booth is there.

[PANELS: the plan, cover-sequence style. Map of an armored-car route, a bank vault, Raul's finger on a timetable, Jason and Lucia's faces in alternating panels]

The plan is simple. A cash pickup on Friday morning, half a million in a single bag, and Boobie's money washes it through the label the same week.

"Easy score," Raul says.

Lucia excuses herself, goes to the bathroom, and texts a number with no name: Friday. Morning. Raul Bautista.

## 6. It goes wrong (6:50-8:10)

[PARALLAX: Vice City causeway at morning rush, layered traffic, helicopters, palm trees streaking past. Jason, Lucia, Raul, Méndez]

Friday comes in hot and bright. The pickup goes clean for exactly ninety seconds.

Then the sirens start, from every direction at once, way too early and way too many.

[PANELS: rapid chase panels. Tires, a shattered windshield, Raul shouting, Lucia's hand on the wheel]

Raul swears in two languages. Jason takes the causeway at a hundred and ten with half a million dollars on the back seat, and Lucia drives the last mile while he leans out the window and empties a magazine at a helicopter he can't hit.

Every time they turn, there's already a cruiser waiting, as if someone handed the police their route.

They lose the cops in Little Haiti. They lose Raul too. He rolls out at a red light with the bag and is gone before Jason can turn around.

In a parking garage, chest heaving, Lucia's burner buzzes in her pocket.

Méndez. "That wasn't the deal," he says, flat as a dial tone. "I wanted Duval in the car with the money. Next time you hand me Jason, or you go back."

She hangs up. Jason is counting what's left in his pockets: three hundred dollars and a dead phone. Twenty-nine days until Brian.

He asks who it was.

"Parole office," she says, and for the first time the lie costs her something.

## 7. Cal finds out (8:10-9:30)

[PARALLAX: roadside motel off the highway, neon VACANCY sign, rain. Cal, Jason, Lucia]

Cal shows up at the motel at two in the morning, soaked, with a cassette in his hand like it's a confession.

He's been recording the police scanner for months. Tonight, between the chase calls, he heard a dispatcher read out an informant code with a name attached.

"C.I. Caminos," Cal says. "They said it three times, Jase. Three times."

[PANELS: the truth sequence. The signature in the cell, the bathroom text, the parking garage call, each panel lit red]

Jason doesn't shout. That's worse. He sits on the edge of the bed and looks at the ankle monitor for a long time.

"How long?"

"Since before the gate," Lucia says. "It was that or eleven more years."

"You could've told me."

"And you'd have done what? Run? You've been running since you were fifteen, Jason."

Jason thinks about the boat, the Coast Guard, the forty grand, and Cal's dates on the wall. Then he does the math he's been avoiding for weeks, and the numbers line up with the day she got out.

"The boat?" he asks.

"No," Lucia says. "That wasn't me. I swear that wasn't me." She has no way to prove it.

Cal stands in the doorway and says what nobody wants to say. "If she's got a handler, the handler knows this room."

Outside, two headlights turn off the highway and stop.

## 8. Only way out (9:30-10:40)

[PARALLAX: Grassrivers swamp at dawn, mist, mangroves, red and blue light bleeding through the trees. Jason, Lucia, Méndez in the background]

They run with no plan, Jason's truck down a dirt road into Grassrivers until the road becomes water. Then they go on foot through the mangroves, with helicopters overhead and the mist turning red and blue.

Lucia's phone rings one more time. Méndez, calm as ever. "Walk him out to the road and you're free. Tonight. No monitor."

She looks at Jason. He's bleeding from the arm and he's still holding the branch back so she can get through.

She has two ways out. One of them is a road with Méndez waiting at the end of it, and the other is wherever Jason is going.

Lucia takes the phone, cracks it in half, and drops it in the black water. Then she bends down, digs a knife under the plastic band on her ankle, and cuts.

"Thought you only made smart moves now," Jason says.

"This is the smartest one I've got."

[PANELS: final cover. Jason and Lucia back to back in the swamp, police lights behind them, title card "ONLY WAY OUT"]

We leave them there, surrounded, holding each other, with sirens on three sides. Maybe that's the end Rockstar planned, and maybe they both walk away. We'll know in six days.

## 9. Close (10:40-10:55)

[PANELS: the eight character cards again, Lucia's second face now revealed]

So tell us in the comments. If you were Lucia, would you have kept the deal?
