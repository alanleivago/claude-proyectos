# Noppera-bo: brief

## Spec
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, **40 s** (960 frames; the user's new maximum). English first-person paragraphs, no voice. Style **yokai**, lit like The Rake: dark, with practical flickering lights.
- **Story:** the classic double scare. theredarchives tells Hearn's "Mujina" (a merchant, the Tokyo moat, a soba stall). Ours moves it to a rainy mountain footpath with a roadside jizō, my grandfather, and my grandmother at the irori. No frames or wording copied.
- **Card:** Flow / Nano Banana Pro. With the style ref attached it returned the ref itself (the Rake print), so I generated without refs and the style described in words. Kept #3 (her face is blank). Seal 無, with a hand grid in `YK_SEALG`.
- **Face:** a blank egg in the first scare. At THE HIT it splits into a vertical mouth with human teeth on red gums (`openMouth`).
