## Claims and sources
- Sunlight takes about 8 min 20 s to reach Earth — NASA (https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/).
- Earth orbits at about 29.8 km/s ≈ 107,000 km/h; without the Sun's gravity it would continue in a straight line — NASA Earth fact sheet (https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html).
- The Moon only shines by reflected sunlight, so it goes dark too — NASA (https://moon.nasa.gov/).
- Surface ~−18 °C (0 °F) within a week and ~−73 °C (−100 °F) within a year — David Stevenson (Caltech), quoted by Live Science, "What would happen if the Sun disappeared?" (https://www.livescience.com/what-if-sun-disappeared).
- Oceans would freeze over at the surface while the deep water stays liquid for a long time — same source.
