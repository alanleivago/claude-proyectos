# single-cinnamon — shot list (beat table v1)

60.5 s, 9:16, 30 fps (1815 frames). Voice: VoiceStudio "3D cartoon 2" (`voice/voice.wav`), runs continuously to 60.46 s
(no wordless gags: every beat is driven by words). Times = word onsets from `voice/words.json`.

**Camera (Ricks sequential rule):** each set is ONE continuous take (`seqCam` / `CAMSEQ[set]`), a key every 1.2-1.8 s,
each leg on a different axis (push / whip-to-subject ON its word / orbit with parallax / crane / dutch roll ±3-8° that
breathes), 3-4 axes at once most of the time, peak angular speed < 110°/s outside whips. Set changes = whip with motion
blur (`grWhip`). 7 takes, 6 whips: canal → street → canal → history → square → street → square.

## Sets
- **canal** — Nyhavn-style quay at golden hour: tall gabled row houses (ochre, brick red, teal, mustard, blue), a canal with
  moored wooden ships and masts, cafe with striped parasols, a cast-iron lamppost on the quay (THE post), bikes, a facade clock,
  Dannebrog flags. Hero tied to the lamppost.
- **street** — residential Copenhagen row-house street: cobbles, bikes in racks, window boxes, a bakery (BAGERI) with a big
  layer cake in the window, a little car that screeches up. Also the ending (a04-a06).
- **history** — an old Danish market town in the 1700s: half-timbered houses, church spire, cobbled market, a road out of town
  with a signpost (RIBE / ODENSE / AARHUS), the pepper man's spice cart (sacks of pepper, cinnamon sticks, scales).
- **square** — a town square: two cast-iron lampposts side by side, Dannebrog bunting, a bakery with a giant cinnamon-roll
  (kanelsnegl) sign, crowd space. d06 = same square at dusk, empty.

## Facts on screen (checked)
- Danish tradition: if you are unmarried on your 25th birthday, friends tie you to a lamppost/tree and throw cinnamon over
  you (often after water so it sticks); at 30 it is pepper. Linked to "pebersvend" (pepper journeyman) / "pebermø" — spice
  salesmen who travelled and stayed single (Visit Denmark, BBC Travel, Atlas Obscura).
