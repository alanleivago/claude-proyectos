In Denmark, if you're still single on your twenty-fifth birthday, your friends do something strange. They tie you to a lamppost… and cover you in cinnamon. You're walking home from work, thinking about cake, and suddenly your best friends jump out of a car. They've got rope. They've got a bucket of water. And they've got an entire bag of cinnamon. Ten minutes later, you're tied to a post in the middle of the street, soaking wet, covered head to toe in spice. A stranger walks past, sniffs, and says, "Happy birthday. Still single, huh?" You see, it's a real tradition, and people say it goes back hundreds of years, to spice sellers who traveled from town to town and were so busy they never settled down. Unmarried men got called "pepper men"… and it stuck. So at twenty-five you get cinnamon, the gentle spice. But if you're still single at thirty? Your friends upgrade you to pepper. And somehow, it's one of the happiest days of the year. The whole street joins in, everyone takes pictures, and you smell like a cinnamon roll for days. So if you're ever in Denmark and someone walks past smelling like dessert… don't ask about their love life. Just say happy birthday.
 Because somewhere out there, a thirty-year-old is still sneezing pepper.
