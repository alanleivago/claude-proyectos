# Ep11 "AVA: How Not to Turn" (guion humanizado, 2a persona)

In a zombie outbreak, the bite isn't what turns you… the X-virus is.
In this world, it spreads like rabies. It's in their blood and spit, and it gets in through a bite, a scratch, or a splash in your eyes or mouth.
Most people fight with bare arms… then one drop of blood hits their eye. A splash counts as a bite.
(YOU DIED)
So cover up. Long sleeves, gloves, goggles, and a mask or a bandana… Blood on a sleeve is just laundry.
If you get bitten or scratched anyway, wash the wound with soap and running water for a full fifteen minutes… then disinfect it… and get real medical help, fast.
After a fight or a cleanup, don't touch your face. Wash your hands first.
In this world, it moves fast. Day one, fever and headache. Day two, red eyes and a blank stare… Day three, you don't want to know.
So anyone who shows up, even the guy at the door, stays alone and watched for seventy-two hours before joining you.
Then you spot a scratch on your arm… when did that happen?… Oh, fudge. (toma del ep1)
So, what do you do?… Wash it for fifteen minutes, or quarantine yourself?… Comment one or two.

Notas: cada "…" es una toma separada. La frase del gancho es la del usuario (contraste "isn't… is" a proposito).
"goggles" sin "or glasses": NIOSH dice que los anteojos comunes no cuentan como proteccion ocular contra salpicaduras.
