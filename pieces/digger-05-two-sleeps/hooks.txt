If you wake up at 3 a.m.… you're not broken. You're medieval.
If you wake up at 3 a.m. every night… your body might be 300 years behind.
For most of history, humans slept twice every night. Then they forgot.
Waking up at 3 a.m. used to be normal. People got up, visited neighbors, then slept again.
Why do you wake up at 3 a.m.? Because 300 years ago, everyone did.
