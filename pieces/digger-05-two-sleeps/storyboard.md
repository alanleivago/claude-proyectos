# Digger 05: storyboard v1

`storyboard_v1.png` shows 23 shots on a provisional timing (no voice yet), about 86 s. The style frames are in `review/styleframes_v1.png` (shots 1, 7, 12, 14 and 22 at full size). The engine is the Expedition 01 engine switched back to 270x480 ×4, with captions using the approved letter spacing (Regular weight, 2 px spacing, hand-drawn digits, no "f " gap).

All sets, props, NPCs and clips are reused from Expedition 01: the night bedroom, the MML2 town at night, the medieval room and straw sack, the candle at midnight, the midnight villagers, the cell desk of diaries, the lab, the sleep lab with a scientist in a lab coat, and the meadow.

New pieces:
- a bedside digital clock;
- the **night bar** (DUSK, 00:00, DAWN), which fills "first sleep", then "second sleep", then merges the two into one (shots 6, 7, 8, 17, 20, 21);
- a hearth;
- a light bulb;
- a vertical Chaucer manuscript page.

## To fix in production (seen on this v1 board)
- **Shots 6 and 8:** the bed fills the vertical frame; frame them wider.
- **Shots 19 and 20:** the volunteers are small; turn the beds 3/4 and use bigger heads (as on the Exp01 thumbnail).
- **Shot 2:** a tight close-up; keep her face clear of the frame edge.
- **Shot 11:** read the hearth tending from the side.
- **Green balance:** the night shots are cool and blue. There are potted plants in every interior and the meadow at the end; production will add ivy and window boxes in the town.
