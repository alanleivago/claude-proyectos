# Digger 05: Humans used to sleep twice (Short)

**Title:** You Wake Up at 3 A.M. Because of This 600-Year-Old Habit
(yt-package 100/100; the cover says "SLEPT TWICE?", no shared words)

**Alternates:** Why Do You Wake Up at 3 A.M.? Blame the 1600s · Humans Used to Sleep Twice Every Night

**Cover:** `portada_1080x1920.png` = `You Wake Up at 3 AM Because of This 600-Year-Old Habit.png` (A: big glowing 3:00 clock + moon, "SLEPT TWICE?"). Variants: `portada_B.png` (awake in bed + clock), `portada_C.png` ("TWO SLEEPS?", Zz | 3:00 | Zz).

**Description:** Waking up at 3 a.m. might not be a problem. For centuries, people slept in two shifts, with an hour awake around midnight. Historian Roger Ekirch found it in hundreds of diaries; a 1992 experiment by Thomas Wehr brought it back. The full bedroom expedition is on the channel.

**Sources:** A. Roger Ekirch, *At Day's Close* (2005) and AHR (2001); Chaucer, *The Squire's Tale*; Wehr, J Sleep Research (1992).
