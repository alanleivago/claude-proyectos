# Odyssey thumbnail: reference analysis

Video: "He Left Troy With 600 Men. He Came Home Alone." (29 min, black-figure vase). Refs in `thumb_refs/`, fonts in `fonts/`, versions in `thumbs/`.

## 1. OuGeoq_uVts — "The Odyssey Explained In 25 Minutes" (The Life Guide, 9.8M views)
- **Composition:** a Renaissance fresco of the Cyclops blinding, cropped to an extreme close-up. The giant's screaming face and arm fill 2/3 of the frame, and the small attacker sits top right. Warm flesh and ochre, mid contrast.
- **Text:** "THE ODYSSEY", huge white grotesque caps (Helvetica/Arial Bold) on two lines, bottom left, flush left, overlapping the art. Closest free font: **Archivo Black**.
- **Why it works:** one violent, instantly readable moment (an eye being stabbed). The scale contrast is giant against man, and the two words are readable at any size. The searchable title words sit right in the image.

## 2. 8Z9FQxcCAZ0 — "Everything you need to know to read Homer's Odyssey" (TED-Ed, 3.9M)
- **Composition:** a flat brick-red field with one black vase-like figure (a traveller with a bundle) standing on books on the right. Text fills the left half. Very high contrast and minimal.
- **Text:** "WHY YOU SHOULD READ / THE ODYSSEY", white hand-drawn Greek-inscription caps (lunate letters, wedge flares) in 3 sizes, centred in the left column. Closest free font: **Caesar Dressing** (Google).
- **Why it works:** it has a single silhouette and a single colour. The type itself says "ancient Greece" and the scale hierarchy guides the eye to THE ODYSSEY.

## 3. ysnzl5fdtMI — "The Ancient Minoans…" (Odyssey channel, 3.3M) — user's favourite
- **Composition:** a rich, highly detailed mosaic portrait of a woman, centred, staring at the viewer. She sits over a fresco frieze full of small scenes and ornament bands. The palette is saturated (teal, gold, terracotta), with a red frame line.
- **Text:** none except the channel bug at the bottom left: a thin circle with a column icon and "ODYSSEY" in widely spaced Trajan-style caps. Closest free font: **Cinzel** (or Marcellus SC).
- **Why it works:** eye contact and a face. The craft and texture feel like a museum piece and promise "premium documentary". The art carries it without a headline, and the round badge reads as a brand seal.

## 4. CVo225pUaSA — "The science behind the myth: Homer's Odyssey" (TED-Ed, 3.2M)
- **Composition:** a ship's prow on the left with the hero standing alone at the rail, set against a huge pale sail and sun disk. The right half is clean pastel sky.
- **Text:** "THE SCIENCE / BEHIND THE / MYTH — HOMER'S / ODYSSEY", stacked, centred, in dark maroon bold Roman caps of alternating sizes, with a white rule between the blocks. Closest free font: **Cinzel** Black (Trajan-like).
- **Why it works:** the lone hero is silhouetted against a light disk. The two-block stacked type with a rule splits the idea into "hook" and "subject", and maroon on light gives crisp contrast.

## 5. Pasted image 1 — Barnes & Noble Signature Editions cover
- **Composition:** a full-bleed red-figure vase scene: Odysseus tied to the mast, Sirens swooping, rowers below, a meander strip at the bottom. Orange on black.
- **Text:** "THE ODYSSEY" in classic large-and-small-caps serif (Goudy/Trajan family), cream, centred over the art with a thin rule and "HOMER" below. Closest free font: **Cormorant SC Bold**.
- **Why it works:** it reads as "the classic, the real book". The type is elegant but legible because it sits on the dark glaze, and the scene is the most famous image in the poem.

## 6. Pasted image 2 — vertical archer poster
- **Composition:** a muted terracotta stone texture, Greek key borders top and bottom, one black-figure archer centred, and a lot of negative space.
- **Text:** "The Odyssey" in a black calligraphic chancery italic with swashes (Aquiline/Fondamento family), two lines, with "The" small above. Closest free font: **Fondamento** (Google).
- **Why it works:** restraint. One figure, one title and a texture: it feels like a museum poster. The handwritten title adds warmth and an "epic" tone.

## Takeaways for our video
- Keep **one hero image** and at most 3–5 words. The hook is the number: **600 → 1**.
- Black figures on terracotta are already our brand. The refs add (a) extreme scale, (b) flat negative space for type, (c) ornate museum richness, and (d) classic serif or calligraphic type in place of the rejected Dalek-style caps.
- Fonts used: Archivo Black, Caesar Dressing, Cinzel (variable, 700–900), Cormorant SC Bold, Fondamento. All are SIL OFL, in `fonts/`.
- The bottom-right corner is kept free of text (YouTube timestamp).
