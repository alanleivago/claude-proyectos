# Odyssey p6 — storyboard (63 panels, 4:59.2)

Piece: pieces/odyssey-p6 · contact sheet: board.jpg · frames: pNN.jpg (each at its key word). Voice: Mad 3 (local TTS, same reference and seeds 7/21 as p3–p5, picked by Whisper score), 62 lines; "AMPHORUS" is not spoken, it is the sign-off card. Sound not done yet (after approval). Starts exactly where p5 ends (Odysseus on the Ithaca beach with the gifts, same camera) on an open panel, so the strip keeps sliding right. Chapter titles 'XIII · ITHACA' and 'XIV · PENELOPE'S BED' stay 1.5 s longer than in p1/p2. New Flow layers in odyssey/cut/p6 (ring + fills to black via odyssey-p6/tools/ring_p6.py). The hall, the palace gate and the rooms slide in as cards; when a card slides over a panel of the same room, a character the new card also shows fades out of the old one, so nobody is on screen twice. Shore, town, sea and night panels slide as one strip (a character in two strip panels in a row stands further right in the second). The slaughter stays in the vase register: men fall and lie still, a dark brown pool, no blood or wounds.

- **P03** (0:12.5–0:19.7) — "So more than a hundred young men from the islands had moved into his palace to court his wife, Penelope." — The hall (card): four suitors walk in (new: Eurymachus, the brute, the archer; Antinous reused); a crowd in a roundel; Penelope at the right.

- **P05** (0:23.8–0:28.7) — "and every day they demanded that she pick one of them to marry." — Eurymachus leans at Penelope; little suns tick by along the top.

- **P08** (0:37.1–0:42.5) — "She worked on it every day, and every night, in secret, she unpicked what she'd woven." — By day the cloth grows; night falls, a lamp, the cloth shrinks.
- **P09** (0:42.5–0:46.6) — "It worked for three years, until one of her maids gave her away." — Three strokes; the maid (new) whispers to Eurymachus; his eye glints.
- **P10** (0:46.6–0:50.9) — "His son, Telemachus, had been a baby when Odysseus sailed for Troy." — Telemachus (new) walks in; a baby in a basket in a roundel turns into the ship sailing.
- **P11** (0:50.9–0:56.9) — "Now he was a grown man, and the suitors were planning to murder him." — The plotters lean together, a knife glints; then the camera pulls back over the whole hall frieze (the suitors, Penelope).

- **P13** (0:59.5–1:07.4) — "His skin wrinkled, his hair fell out, and his eyes went cloudy, and she dressed him in filthy rags so that nobody would know him." — He turns into the old beggar (crossfade in the sparks), a pale film over the eye.
