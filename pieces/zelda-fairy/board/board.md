# zelda-fairy: storyboard (14 panels, 1:05.8)

Piece: pieces/zelda-fairy. Contact sheet: board.jpg. Frames: pNN.jpg, each at its key word. Mid-slide checks: mid/mid_slides.jpg.
Voice: Mad 3 (local TTS, Odyssey reference, seeds 7/21 picked by Whisper score), speed 0.9, 16 lines. No sound or music yet (the user brings the music).
Look (v4, 2026-11-02): the top and bottom bands are a woven figure frieze (forest children, trees, fairies, Triforce, mushrooms; Flow from the Zelda3 band, one seamless period tiled twice, cut in a gap between motifs). No side bands. The characters are crude woven Bayeux/hieroglyph figures, generated WITHOUT character names ("a boy in a green tunic", "a tall desert warlord"), using only the ref crops (sleeping Link, Zelda3 tapestry). The tree, forest, castle and temple are redone from the Zelda3/Zelda4 refs (swirling woven trunks, a woven castle). Layers: gen4/. Comparison sheet: look/v4_ref_vs_characters.jpg.
Link is one actor on the strip. When two panels in a row show him he is drawn once, walks across the seam, and his pose crossfades. Navi rides with him from P06.

## Panels
- **P01** (0:00.0–0:03.0): "You know the Hero of Time. But before the sword," Adult Link in a burst of gold, leaves falling. At the slide he fades into the boy.
- **P02** (0:03.0–0:07.7): "Link was the one Kokiri boy with no fairy, and the forest never let him forget it." The Kokiri forest. Mido walks in, arms crossed, with his fairy circling, and laughs on "forget". Link stands sad.
- **P03** (0:07.7–0:10.7): "Every other child in those woods had a fairy of their own." The girl's fairy swoops past Link. He reaches up (pose crossfade), and it flies back to her.
- **P04** (0:10.7–0:13.9): "Link just had the same bad dream, night after night." Night falls across the seam (navy wash with feathered edges, woven stars). Link sleeps on a branch. The dream sits in a roundel: Ganondorf before the castle under a red sky.
- **P05** (0:13.9–20.3): "Then the Great Deku Tree… fell sick. A man from the desert had put a curse on him." The tree breathes and greys and drops leaves. Ganondorf walks in from the left, and plum curse strands crawl from his hand to the tree's face.
- **P06** (0:20.3–0:24.3): "So the tree finally sent the boy a fairy. Her name was Navi." Link sleeps on a branch by the forest. Navi flies in from the right, and on "name" he wakes and stands, reaching up to her.
- **P07** (0:24.3–0:28.8): "Link climbed inside the tree and broke the curse, but it had already done its work." Link walks into the tree's mouth and shrinks away. Gold rays break the curse, he comes back out, and the tree goes grey as its leaves pour down.
- **P08** (0:28.8–0:34.1): "Before he died, the tree told him to leave the forest and find the princess at Hyrule Castle." Link walks out of the thinning forest with Navi, past a woven signpost pointing right.
- **P09** (0:34.1–0:41.2): "Princess Zelda had seen him in her dreams. When the man from the desert came after her, she threw Link the Ocarina of Time." Hyrule Castle with Zelda in front. Ganondorf walks in behind her with a dark glow. The ocarina, one sprite, arcs from her hands to Link's raised hand.
- **P10** (0:41.2–0:45.4): "He played it in the Temple of Time and pulled the Master Sword from its stone." The Temple of Time set fills the cloth. Link plays (woven notes rise), then crossfades to holding up the Master Sword on "pulled", with gold rays.
- **P11** (0:45.4–0:49.9): "He was too young to carry it, so the sword sealed him away for seven years." Link sleeps, floating in light. Seven woven tally marks stitch in on "seven years".
- **P12** (0:49.9–0:54.9): "When Link woke up, he was a grown man, and the man from the desert ruled Hyrule." The sleeper fades into adult Link. A red-brown wash darkens Hyrule as Ganondorf rises huge behind the darkened castle.
- **P13** (0:54.9–1:00.8): "Soon you'll get to walk that whole road again, starting in the forest, on the night he had no fairy." Back in the bright forest: young Link walks with Navi while the girl and her fairy wave.
- **P14** (1:00.8–1:05.8): Sign-off, not spoken. The apple sits higher, with the cream woven plaque carrying AMPHORUS in gold right under it, and below that, small: "Beyond time, the boy becomes a hero".
