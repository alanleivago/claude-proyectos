# Polvo de estrellas — brief

**Spec:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 60 s, 120 BPM. Music + box captions, Spanish. Style `pixdoc`, space variant (360×640 world, box captions, element colours).
**Format:** F2 chronology in one continuous zoom; beats joined by pixel-mosaic zooms. Adapted from the "Stars Made 90% of You" reference: our own hook/ending (a blood drop and its iron atom), Suns for scale, He and Ne shells, an accelerating clock, the body-composition bar, the person and the blood callback.
**Device:** one colour per element for the whole piece; iron = rust red = blood.

## Claims and sources
- Stars > ~8 M☉ end in iron-core collapse; iron fusion yields no energy; the core collapses in < 1 s — [Janka 2012](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.2503).
- Burning stages: He ~10⁵ yr, C centuries, Ne ~1 yr, O months, Si days (25 M☉; varies by model) — [LCO](https://lco.global/spacebook/stars/high-mass-star), [Silicon burning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon-burning_process), [MESA on model scatter](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1009.1622).
- Body ≈ 65 % O, 18 % C, 10 % H; the hydrogen dates from the Big Bang, so ~90 % of your mass was made in stars — [Dr Karl, Australian Geographic](https://www.australiangeographic.com.au/?p=324670).
- The Sun formed ~4.6 billion years ago (standard value).
- Iron atom: 26 protons, shells 2-8-14-2 (standard).
