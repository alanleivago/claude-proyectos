# Ganaste esa carrera — brief

- **Spec:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 59.5 s (the voice is the clock), 120 BPM grid, style pixdoc, narrator (user's single Spanish take cut into 14 lines at silences, pauses lengthened for the picture).
- **Source:** adapted from reference `Ref posit pixel/6.mp4` ("You won that race"; music + captions, no voice): Spanish, reworded, extended (perspective tunnel, the lost/stuck/wrong-way rivals, the seal wave and SELLADO stamp, the heart loupe, 1-in-250-million ending).
- **Device:** a counter falls (RIVALES 250.000.000 → ≈300 → GANADOR 1) and then rises (DÍA, CÉLULAS 1→16, SEMANA 7→38). Yellow = you.

## Music (own)
Two colours. Race: B minor (Bm–G–A–F#), gallop bass (dum-da-da each beat), racing 16th arp, four-on-the-floor. After "Tú": D major (D–A–Bm–G), heartbeat bass (lub-dub), music-box arp with echo, lullaby lead; heartbeat thumps in the SFX from the loupe on. Mix: voice + music −9 dB (sidechain) + SFX +4 dB (light duck) → −14 LUFS.

## Claims and sources

- Day 5 blastocyst (hollow ball); implantation ~day 6–12; heart begins beating ~day 22 (estimates 18–22).
