# Ep4 "AVA: The Last Train — Part 1" (guion humanizado)

Imagine you miss the last train… and the station locks you in.
Your phone has no signal and three percent battery. It's eleven forty-two… and the next train isn't until five thirty.
Then you find a sheet taped up in the ticket booth, night shift instructions. Rule one says never board an empty train.
Rule two is torn off halfway through… if you hear footsteps behind you, don't… and that's where the paper ends.
You laugh it off. It's probably the staff pranking the new guy.
At eleven fifty-nine you're still waiting. When the clock hits midnight… every light in the station goes out.
A train pulls in with two headlights… and it doesn't make a sound. The doors slide open on an empty car.
Then you hear slow footsteps behind you. Something is standing right there… and you can feel it breathing on your neck.
Oh, fudge. And the doors start closing.
So do you get on… or do you stay?
