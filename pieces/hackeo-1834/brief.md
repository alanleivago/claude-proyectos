# El primer hackeo — brief

**Spec:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 60 s, 120 BPM (half-time feel). Music + typed captions, no voice. Spanish.
**Style:** `pixdoc` (new, made from the "Ref posit pixel" references): a 270×480 world (4 px pixels), text on 180×320 (6 px), navy subtitle bar typed on, amber labels, gilded loupes, static paper/stone grain, dithered skies, a floating camera with depth parallax.
**Format:** F4 mission on a documentary stage — hard cuts between beats, loupes (iris insets) inside them. Unproven combination before this piece.
**Device:** the fake symbol is always RED; it travels tower to tower. The ending returns to the opening roof at dusk, its arms red.
**Source:** structure adapted from reference 4 (Spanish script, our own wording, hook, ending and extra scenes: the codebook finger, the coach's day counter, the operator falling ill, the law on paper, towers becoming masts).

## Claims and sources
- Bordeaux bankers François and Louis Blanc (twins, b. 1806) gamed the state Chappe telegraph from 1834, bribing operators at Tours; a Paris accomplice sent parcels whose garment signalled the market's direction; the fake symbol was cancelled with the network's error/backspace sign so the official copy stayed clean; a spy read the last tower near Bordeaux. — [Schneier](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2018/05/1834_the_first_.html), [Andrea Fortuna](https://andreafortuna.org/2018/06/15/stock-market-fraud-steganography-and-cyberattacks-in-1834-the-fascinating-tale-of-blanc-brothers/), [Fluid Attacks](https://fluidattacks.com/blog/first-cyberattack/), [Mark Pack](https://markpack.org.uk/156276/francois-joseph-blanc-semaphore-scam)
- Names François and Louis, twins born 1806 — [Wikipedia: François Blanc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Blanc), [Universalis](https://www.universalis.fr/encyclopedie/blanc-francois-et-louis/)
- Bordeaux got Paris prices days late by mail coach — Schneier / Fortuna above.
- Discovered in 1836 when the Tours operator fell ill and told a friend; trial at the Tours assizes from 11 March 1837; no law covered misuse of the telegraph — [hard2bit](https://hard2bit.com/en/blog/first-cyberattack-in-history-chappe-telegraph/), Schneier.
- Law of 2 May 1837: unauthorised transmission of signals punished with prison and a fine — [Musée de La Poste](https://www.museedelaposte-lcl.net.extra.laposte.fr/system/files/fp_ptt.pdf)
- **Illustrative, not sourced:** the codebook word (PAZ), the price 79,80 → 80,05, the day count to 5, the garment drawn (purple cloth). Gloves/stockings as the code was NOT verified and is not claimed.
