## Claims and sources
- The equator moves at about 1,670 km/h (Earth's circumference / 24 h) — NASA / Astronomy magazine (https://www.astronomy.com/science/what-happens-if-earth-stops-rotating/).
- If the planet stopped, the atmosphere would keep moving at about that speed: winds over 1,600 km/h, far beyond the strongest measured gust (407 km/h, Barrow Island) — Weatherzone (https://weatherzone.com.au/news/what-would-happen-if-earth-stopped-rotating/1726270).
- That is faster than sound (~1,235 km/h at sea level).
- The oceans would also keep moving and surge over the land — same sources.
