## Description
Under the graveyard of Kakariko, Ocarina of Time hides the Shadow Temple, and its wall reads: "Here is gathered Hyrule's bloody history of greed and hatred." Guillotines, spiked walls, invisible floors, the Lens of Truth at the bottom of the well, Dead Hand, Impa's seal and Bongo Bongo, told as a woven night tapestry.

What the game shows and says is stated as fact; who was kept in those cells is never named, so the "dirty work of the royal family" part is a fan theory, and the video says so.
