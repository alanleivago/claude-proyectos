## Narration (plain, for the voice)
You walked through Kakariko a hundred times, but you never asked why its dead are buried on top of a temple full of guillotines.
Kakariko began as a village of the Sheikah, the shadows who guarded Hyrule's royal family.
Under the graveyard sits the Shadow Temple. A wall inside reads: "Here is gathered Hyrule's bloody history of greed and hatred."
Blades drop from the ceiling, spiked walls close in, and some floors are invisible.
To see them you need the Lens of Truth, hidden under the village well, where a pale thing called Dead Hand waits.
Impa sealed an evil shadow in that well. When it broke loose, Kakariko burned, and Bongo Bongo waited in the temple below.
The game never says who suffered down there. Many fans think the Sheikah did the royal family's dirty work in those rooms.
That part is theory. The words on that wall come from the game itself.

## Lore check
- In the game: Kakariko as a Sheikah village (Impa opened it to everyone), the Sheikah as the royal family's shadow guards (also Hyrule Historia), the Shadow Temple under the graveyard (Nocturne of Shadow), the inscription "Here is gathered Hyrule's bloody history of greed and hatred...", guillotine blades, closing spiked walls, invisible floors and the Lens of Truth, the Lens in the Bottom of the Well with Dead Hand, Sheik's line that the evil spirit Impa sealed in the well broke free (Kakariko burns), Bongo Bongo as the Shadow Temple boss.
- Marked as theory in the narration and with an on-screen FAN THEORY tag: that the rooms were used for torture/executions by the Sheikah for the royal family. The game never names who was held there; the well spirit = Bongo Bongo is implied, not stated by name.
