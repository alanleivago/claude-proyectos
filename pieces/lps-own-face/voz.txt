You have never seen your own face. Not once. Every mirror you've ever looked into flipped it, so the face you know is backwards. Photos don't help either. Held at arm's length, a phone lens can make your nose look about thirty percent wider. You see, your brain loves the version it sees the most. In 1977, researchers showed people two photos of themselves. The real one, and a mirrored one. They picked the mirrored one. Their friends picked the real one. That's why your photos look wrong to you. They're the face everyone else gets. The closest you'll ever come is the tip of your nose. It's in everything you look at. Your brain just deletes it.
