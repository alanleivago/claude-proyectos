# zaw-dog #5: shot plan

The camera follows the "Planos" rules in [STYLE.md](../../../../styles/zawdog/STYLE.md), with the user's corrections from video 4:
- varied heights, with at most one low angle;
- the camera MOVES in every shot, with eased moves that change from shot to shot;
- no two shots in a row share both size and angle.

"Level" means the camera sits at the dog's eye height (0.42-0.5 m) and points horizontally.

## Counts (17 shots)
- **Camera height and angle:**
  - level at dog height: 9 (53%);
  - gentle high angle: 4 (24%);
  - human height, medium or wide: 3 (18%);
  - top-down inserts: 1 (6%);
  - low angle: 0. The rule allows one; this script doesn't need it.
- **Movement:** all 17 shots move, so none is static.
  - push-in: 6 (01a, 02b, 05, 07, 12, 14a);
  - pull-out: 2 (04, 13);
  - track or dolly: 5 (02a, 06, 09, 10, 14b);
  - orbit: 2 (01b, 03);
  - pan: 1 (11);
  - crane: 1 (08).
  No two shots in a row use the same move. The closest pair is 09 and 10: both move sideways, but 09 tracks a level dog at 0.48 m and 10 dollies at 1.4 m.
- **Sizes:** 7 medium, 5 full, 2 close, 2 wide, 1 insert.
- **Consecutive shots:** no pair shares both size and angle.
  - 02a and 02b are both medium, but one is a high angle and the other level.
  - 09 and 10 are both full, but one is level and the other a high angle.
  - 14a and 14b are both medium, but one is at human height and the other level.
- **Loop:** 14b (level, medium, the dog in the grass) cuts to 01a (level, full, the dog at the post), so the size changes at the seam.
