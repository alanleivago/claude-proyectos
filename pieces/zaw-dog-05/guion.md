# You Should Let Your Dog Sniff Everything on Walks (zaw-dog #5)

EN, about 36 s, ~120 words, deadpan narrator, one cut per sentence (14 cuts). It follows Zaw's formula "You Should Let Your Cat Knock Things Off The Table" (16.8M), a counter-intuitive permission. PawPoly has not done it: its "doggy handshake" short is about butt sniffing, not walks.

**Loop:** line 14 runs into line 1 ("…just stop and / let your dog sniff everything"). **Hero bark:** in the comma gap after "everything,". **Cast:** the approved v2 dog and the approved owner.

**Camera:** varied angles per the "Planos" section of styles/zawdog/STYLE.md, with the camera MOVING in every shot.

## Fact check (2026-11-10)

**Duranton & Horowitz (2019),** "Let me sniff! Nosework induces positive judgment bias in pet dogs", *Applied Animal Behaviour Science* 211:61-66 (doi 10.1016/j.applanim.2018.12.009), Barnard College Dog Cognition Lab. It covers lines 5-13:
- **Sample:** 20 pet dogs, at least 1 year old.
- **Bowl training:** the dogs learned that a bowl in one location always had food and a bowl in another never did.
- **Test:** an ambiguous bowl placed in between.
- **Activity:** then two weeks of daily activity, with 10 dogs on nosework and 10 on heelwork.
- **Result:** afterwards the nosework dogs reached the ambiguous bowl significantly faster. The heelwork dogs did not change.
- **Wording:** the authors call it more "optimistic".
- **Caveat:** small sample. The script says "more optimistic", not "happier forever".

**Receptors (lines 3-4):** the commonly cited figures are "up to 300 million" olfactory receptors in dogs against about 6 million in humans (Horowitz; PBS NOVA). They vary by breed, hence "around".

**Message (line 2):** dogs use urine scent marks to communicate. "Reading the news" is a common metaphor.
