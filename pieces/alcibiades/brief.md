## Claims and sources
- Raised in Pericles' household; Socrates' pupil — FHW (fhw.gr/chronos/05/en/politics/450alcibiades.html), worldhistory.org/Alcibiades
- Led the case for the Sicilian expedition (415 BC), co-commander — FHW; Ancient World Magazine
- Herms mutilated before sailing, blamed; recalled, escaped at Thurii, went to Sparta — FHW; Thucydides 6
- Advised Sparta to help Syracuse and fortify Decelea — FHW; Ancient World Magazine
- Affair with Timaea, wife of King Agis II (ancient rumour, in Plutarch) — Ancient World Magazine
- Fled to the satrap Tissaphernes, played both sides, promised Persian support to Athens — FHW
- Recalled, won (Cyzicus 410), returned to Athens 407 — worldhistory.org
- Killed in Phrygia c. 404 BC: house set on fire, shot with javelins and arrows as he ran out, dagger in hand — Plutarch, Alc. 39 (Perseus)
