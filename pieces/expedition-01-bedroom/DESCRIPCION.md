**Description:**

Thousands of years from now, Mechayuki digs up a buried bedroom and finds eight strange human artifacts, all for the same activity: lying still for eight hours. She follows each one back in time. Grass beds on a layer of ash 200,000 years ago, stone pillows, strangers sharing beds at inns, Adams and Franklin fighting over a window, the "first sleep" and "second sleep", pajamas from Persia, the toilet hiding inside the nightstand, Odysseus's beeswax earplugs. And at the end, one artifact she can't explain.

What was it for? Tell her in the comments.
