## Sound effects and ambience

Real recordings from Freesound.org and Wikimedia Commons, trimmed, layered, level-adjusted, equalised, panned and pitch-shifted by resampling for the film; in the barrel (469-497.5) three of them (the Ionian Sea crash, "Seawaves against rocks", the spillway) are also run through a code-made tube impulse response (tools/tube.py). On top of the recordings sits a code-synthesized presence layer made for this film (tools/synth.py, seeded: the teaser drone, the wave's low rumble, whooshes, the space drone and glass tones, the ocean-drain swell, the riser, the Moon hit's sub drop / crack / tail and a low barrel roar); it uses no third-party audio and needs no credit.

Licence audit: every recording above was checked on its own record on 2026-10-25 (Freesound API sound page / Commons file page / incompetech FAQ); `assets/manifests/audit.json` + `audit.md` (real-sound audit.py: 46 of 46 used files ok, 0 blocked; licences used: CC0 32, CC BY 4.0 11 incl. the music, CC BY 3.0 3; no NC / ND / SA).
