## Claims + sources
- Horizon distance ≈ 3.57·√h km (h in m): ~14 km from 15 m — SDSU (aty.sdsu.edu/explain/atmos_refr/horizon.html).
- Gravity pulls large bodies into spheres (hydrostatic equilibrium); the threshold is roughly 400 km for icy and ~600 km for rocky bodies and depends on composition — Wikipedia "Geophysical definition of planet"; Asterism (Lineweaver & Norman).
- Illustrative: a disc Earth's gravity tilting toward the centre, the ocean held at the rim, the rim curling up (thought experiment, as in the reference), the HUD numbers.
