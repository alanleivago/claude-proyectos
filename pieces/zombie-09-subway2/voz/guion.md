# Ep9 "AVA: The Last Train — Part 2" (guion humanizado)

Adapta la parte 2 del reel "Subway" de play.malo (`fuentes/zombie-nicho/playmalo-subway-p2.md`), parafraseado. Segunda persona.

Last time, you had to choose… get on, or stay.
Say you stay. The doors close, and the train leaves without you. On the floor you find the rest of rule two… if you hear footsteps behind you, don't turn around.
And right then, you hear footsteps. So you turn around. Two eyes in the dark… and a smile way too wide for a face.
You run for the exit, but the gate is still locked.
Oh, fudge.
Okay, let's rewind.
This time, you get on. The doors shut right in its face, and it bangs on the glass… but you're inside. You finally breathe.
Then the train speeds up. It switches tracks and stops in a tunnel you've never seen… and the lights go out.
A screen turns on. Welcome to the last stop, you have one minute. Red, you leave the train. White, you stay on board.
Then the doors open… on total darkness. Five seconds left… and something out there is breathing.
So, what do you do? Leave… or stay? Comment one or two.
