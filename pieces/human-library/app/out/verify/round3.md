**Fixes**
1. **117, 1832:** Characters are cut by the frame edge. In 117, pull the camera back about 15% and centre the librarian. In 1832, move right so the right character's head and the full BORROWED sign fit inside the frame.
2. **1118, 1006:** In 1118, reframe so the woman sits fully in frame with lead room. In 1006, recentre so the right-hand woman's head is not clipped.
3. **68, 1352, 1537:** Label cards (e.g. "COVERED IN TATTOOS") are unreadable at phone size and get clipped ("REFUGE", "TER"). Scale them about 2× and keep them inside the frame.
4. **334:** The top of the catalog card is soft, so the "Cooking for Beginners" line is illegible. Put the whole card in focus and lighten the strike line.
5. **661, 764, 1448, 1631, 1741:** Interiors are flat with no emissive lamps. Make the green lamps light sources (warm pools on the desks), add contact shadows under chairs and characters, and add a soft rim light from the windows.
6. **885, 418:** Close-ups lack a focus point. Put focus on the eyes. In 885, add bokeh to the shelves; in 418, nudge the face up so the chin isn't crowded.
7. **19, 1252, 1195:** Exterior lighting is flat. Add a sun direction and shadows, use horizon-coloured fog in 1195, and tone down the cyan water.
8. **Bookshelf frames:** The shelves show obvious tiling. Vary the book colours and heights, and add ~3 px ink on the shelf edges.
9. **Seated shots:** Characters read at about 2.5 head-heights. Lengthen torsos toward 3.5.
