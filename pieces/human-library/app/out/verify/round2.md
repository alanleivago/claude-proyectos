**Fixes (most important first)**

1. **Frame 510:** The card is cut at the left edge and the librarian at the right. Pull the camera back about 20%, centre the card, and keep her full head and shoulders in frame.
2. **Frame 1440:** This extreme close-up crops the head top and the body. Widen to a medium close-up so the whole head, plus a 10% margin, is in frame, and focus on the eyes.
3. **Frame 1690:** The librarian is clipped by the right edge and the chair back. Shift her 15% inward and lower the chair.
4. **Frame 75:** The table signs ("Police Officer", "Refugee") are unreadable at phone size. Scale the sign cards about 2.5x or push in to a closer wide shot.
5. **Frames 120–1530 (library interior):** The lighting is flat. Add a warm key light from the lamps and window, soft cast shadows under the tables and characters, and a cooler fill. This would match the reference contrast without crushing the blacks.
6. **Frames 75–1690:** The bookshelf texture is a noisy, repeating stripe pattern that creates moiré. Reduce the spine count, enlarge the books, add colour-block variation, and put roughly 3 px ink on the shelf edges.
7. **Frame 120:** The character hides the "LOANS" sign. Move him left so the sign stays readable.
8. **Frame 330:** Enlarge the catalog card so all four entries sit clear of the subtitle zone.
9. **Frame 20:** The figures are tiny. Lower the camera and add one foreground character.
