**Fixes**

1. **Lighting (all interiors: 68, 117, 661–1118, 1448–1832):** The light is flat and shadowless. Add a warm key from the green lamps or windows, cool fill, contact shadows under chairs and desks, and a soft light pool on the rug. Target day median luma ~.57.
2. **Edge cuts (117, 509, 661, 1006, 1118, 1832):** Characters are sliced by the frame edge. Reframe or dolly out so each subject has ~5% margin. In 1118, recentre on the woman instead of the lamp.
3. **Close-ups (418, 885):** The head overfills the frame. Pull back to chest-up, put the eyes on the upper third, and focus on the eyes.
4. **Text cards (68, 1352, 1537, 509):** The category labels and desk card are unreadable at phone size. Scale them 2–3×, use a bolder font, and cut to an insert. In 334, show the "Police Officer" card fully.
5. **Exteriors (19, 1195, 1252):** Add horizon-colour fog, warm sun with cast shadows, and richer stone/cobble chroma. Keep the wides sharp.
6. **Textures (all bookshelf shots):** The shelf tile repeats visibly. Vary spine heights and colours, add wood grain and grime, and give the rug a weave.
7. **World ink (68, 1537):** Bring silhouette and crease lines on desks, shelves and the architecture up to ~3 px near-black.
8. **Characters (117, 1832):** Bodies are too tall. Rescale to ~3.5 head-heights with plum ink and stick limbs.
