**Fixes**

1. **1350:** The sky and fog wash out to near-white, and the scene is flat and empty. Tint the fog to the horizon blue, lower the exposure toward 0.57 median luma, and add ground and building detail behind the wall.
2. **75–1830 (all interiors):** The lighting is flat and ambient, with no key light. Add a warm key light through the windows, pools of light under the green lamps, and soft contact shadows under the desks and chairs.
3. **575, 1125, 890, 1830:** Characters are cut by the frame edges. Reframe so the librarian (575, 1125), the bald man (890) and both characters (1830) sit fully inside the frame with headroom.
4. **75, 1530, 1350:** The label cards ("POLICE OFFICER", "REFUGEE") are illegible at phone size. Double the card size or push the camera in.
5. **330:** The catalog text runs off the right edge and is tilted. Straighten it, pull back, and keep the struck-through title fully visible.
6. **Bookshelves (all interiors):** The tiled texture repeats and the shelves lack ink. Add per-shelf variation and about 3 px near-black ink on the shelf edges and wall corners.
7. **420, 890, 1740:** On these close-ups the background is too sharp. Add lens blur with focus on the eyes. Keep 1190 and 75 sharp.
8. **1190:** The aerial shot is grey and hazy. Raise chroma, and lift the river and grass toward rich, non-neon values.
9. **Walls:** The walls are flat beige. Add a subtle panel or plaster texture.
