# 1,000 Years of Getting Lost Before GPS

Cover text: WHICH WAY?

How did anyone get home before GPS? An android tries every trick: steering by the North Star, Marshall Islands stick charts, Roman milestones, the Song dynasty compass, hand-drawn sea charts, John Harrison's 1761 sea clock and the sextant… then the satellites, whose clocks run 38 microseconds a day fast because of Einstein.
