# Digger 01: The fridge (v2, trimmed ~34 words, target ~75 s)

Operator… I dug up another human artifact. Humans called it… a fridge.

If you dig a hole in the snow and throw your meat in it… you have a refrigerator.

But snow melts in summer… so humans cut giant blocks of ice from frozen lakes… and hid them in a shed full of sawdust. This is an icehouse.

But ice melts… and your meat ends up swimming. Gross.

So they put the ice on top of a wooden box… and the food underneath. Cold air sinks. This is an icebox.

Operator Mike asks: "Why not just make cold?" Mike… you can't make cold. You can only steal heat.

When a liquid evaporates… it steals heat from everything around it. That's why you shiver after a shower.

So they filled a pipe with a special liquid… that boils at minus twenty-six degrees. This is a refrigerant.

Inside, it boils… and steals your food's heat.

Then a pump squeezes it until it's hot… and dumps the heat out the back. This is a compressor.

That's why the back of your fridge is always warm.

So your fridge is a heat thief… running in a loop, forever.

Humans built a machine… just to rob their own leftovers?

Artifact understood. Logging off.
