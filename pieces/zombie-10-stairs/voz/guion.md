# Ep10 "AVA: The Stairs" (guion humanizado, 2a persona)

In a zombie outbreak, your safe room won't hold forever… so you go up.
Most people stay downstairs, with two doors and five windows to guard at once.
Then the front door gives, and they pour in… and you're too late for the stairs.
(YOU DIED)
In this world, zombies are terrible at stairs… So you go up. The staircase becomes your wall, only one can climb at a time.
First, you block the bottom steps. Tip the sofa, stack the dresser on it… and screw them together with three-inch screws. You already know how.
Then the top. You screw a door across the stairs on its edge, and push a heavy shelf against it.
But don't trap yourself. Your exit is the upstairs window, with an escape ladder… Fire safety says every room needs two ways out.
You carry up the water, the backpack, the radio, and the First Aid Spray… Day four, you save your game.
Then someone knocks downstairs. Zombies don't knock.
