# Ep13 "AVA: Gas Station, Part 1" (guion humanizado, 2a persona)

It's 2 AM, and you're alone at a gas station, filling up… Then the lights flicker, and the pump stops at twelve forty.
Your tank's only a quarter full, and the hose is dead… Gas pumps run on electricity, so no power means no gas.
So you head into the shop and grab what matters… Water before snacks, then a flashlight and some bandages… You can last weeks without food, but only about three days without water.
Don't hide by the windows. Glass breaks, and they can see you through it… The walk-in cooler has one door, thick walls, and no windows. That's your safe room.
Outside, shapes cross in front of a car's headlights… A flashlight behind all that glass can be seen from the road… Flashlight off. Stay low, and don't move.
Someone knocks from inside the locked bathroom…
(DESCONOCIDO b0469a54, apagado por la puerta) Please… let me in. I'm not bitten… I cut my hand on the glass.
A hand slides out under the door, wrapped in a bandage.
A drop of blood rolls out after it… Oh, fudge. (toma del ep1) … Bites bleed, but so do cuts.
So, would you unlock it?… Unlock it, or leave it locked?… Comment one or two.
