## Descripción (copiar/pegar)

Day 2 inside the old police station. Vickers knocked on the glass all night, and at sunrise you crack his code. Then the problems start, hour by hour: a generator that lights you up like a lighthouse, a bucket toilet, a prisoner named Dutch in cell four, a clover key, a 1920 station buried under the floor, a tunnel… and a helicopter that leaves a note: BRIDGE. DAY 3. DAWN. ONE SEAT.

Do you let Dutch out? Comment YES or NO. Most comments decide Day 3.

Real survival facts in this video: a portable generator burns about half a gallon an hour, and you never run it indoors (CDC); bucket toilet with two bags plus a dry layer (FEMA / Ready.gov); 1 gallon of water per person per day; how to check canned food (bulging, leaking or rusted = throw it out, USDA); first aid for a bite is soap and running water for 15 minutes (CDC/WHO); three of anything = distress signal; a signal mirror flash can be seen from about 10 miles away.
