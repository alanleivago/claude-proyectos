# AVA: NIGHT 1 — The Police Station (versión larga, 16:9, ~9 min)

Título YouTube: **Zombie Outbreak: 1 Night Locked Inside a Police Station**
Estructura (fuentes/zombie-nicho/largo/ANALISIS.md): lista de problemas con un dato real cada uno, guardado a máquina como corte de capítulo,
puerta de carga entre salas, pantalla ITEM cada ~90 s, contador de zombis en el cristal, misterio de la radio sembrado al principio y
pagado al final, compañero (perro K-9) a mitad, final en espejo con el menú YES/NO. ~1250 palabras ≈ 8:40 a 145 ppm.
Datos: FEMA 1 gal/persona/día; cisterna 1.6 gal; calentador 40–50 gal; disparo 9 mm ~160 dB; generador nunca dentro (CO, CDC);
cristal templado se rompe entero en trocitos; perro oye ~4 veces más lejos.

## 0:00 — Gancho
Zombies took the city at nine… and you're locked inside the old police station.
Sounds like the safest place in town… it isn't.
By sunrise you'll have one dog, fifteen bullets… and a very good reason not to open that door.

## 9:02 PM — How you got here
Here's how it happened. You ran out of gas two blocks away… and the only building with its lights still on was this one.
The front doors were open. Nobody at the desk. Coffee still warm.
So you did what anyone would do… you locked the gate behind you. Click.
And that's when the radio on the front desk started talking.

## 9:15 PM — The radio
[RADIO, voz robótica] Emergency broadcast. Help arrives in seven days. Lights off. Gate locked. Open for no one.
Seven days. Okay. You can do seven days.
But here's the weird part… the message ends, and twenty minutes later… it plays again. Same words. Same pause. Same cough at the end.
Remember that cough.

## 9:40 PM — Problem one: the glass
First problem… the front doors are glass.
And not the movie kind. This is tempered glass. It takes a lot to crack it… but once it goes, the whole thing comes down at once. Thousands of little pieces. No warning.
So, rule one… you never, ever go near the lobby.
You push the benches against the inner doors, you kill every light you can see from the street… and you count.
Three of them outside. Just… standing there.

## 10:15 PM — Problem two: the armory
Good news… you found the armory.
Bad news… someone found it first.
One pistol. Fifteen bullets. That's it.
And honestly? That's fine. Because a nine-millimeter shot is about a hundred and sixty decibels… louder than a jet taking off right next to you.
Every bullet you fire is a dinner bell. For the whole block.
So, rule two… the gun is the last thing you use. Not the first.

## 10:50 PM — Problem three: water
But bullets aren't what gets you first… water is.
You need about one gallon a day, just to drink and stay clean. That's FEMA's number, not mine.
The water cooler has five. Four toilet tanks… that's six more, as long as nobody flushes.
And then there's the one everybody forgets… the water heater in the basement. Forty gallons, just sitting there.
Fifty-one gallons. Fifty-one days. Help comes in seven.
For the first time tonight… you smile.

## 11:30 PM — The kennel
Then you hear it. Scratching. Behind the door marked K-9.
Every movie says don't open it. You open it.
And there she is… a German shepherd, half asleep, wagging her tail like she's been waiting all night.
Her tag says RIBBON.
And Ribbon is better than any alarm you could build… a dog can hear some sounds about four times farther away than you can.
So now, when she growls… you listen.

## 12:40 AM — Problem four: power
Around midnight, the backup power dies.
There's a generator in the basement… and here's the part that kills more people than zombies do.
Never run a generator inside. Not in the basement, not in the garage, not with the door open. Carbon monoxide has no smell, no color… you just get sleepy, and you don't wake up.
So the generator stays off. Which means rule three makes even more sense… lights off. All of them.
Ribbon doesn't mind. She sleeps on your boots.

## 2:47 AM — The save room
You find a little room with no windows, one door, and a typewriter.
If you've ever played a certain kind of game… you know exactly what this is.
You save your game. Day one… two forty-seven A.M. Police station.
The radio plays again in the hall. Help arrives in seven days. Cough.
Same cough.

## 3:14 AM — The knock
At three fourteen, someone knocks on the glass.
Ribbon doesn't bark. She just stands up… and stares.
[COP] Police department. We're evacuating the block. Open the door.
A uniform. A cap. A badge. Rule four… and you're writing this one down right now… a badge is not a password.
Then you check the duty log. Every officer on shift tonight… status: lost.
Including Officer Vickers.
And here's the thing… the zombies outside? Three became eight. Eight became twenty. They're following his knocking.

## 3:40 AM — The lights
He's still knocking. Then every light in the station turns on… by itself.
Oh, fudge.
Somebody started the generator. And it wasn't you.
You run to the radio room… and there it is. A tape recorder, plugged into the transmitter. Playing on a loop.
Help arrives in seven days. Cough.
Nobody is coming in seven days. That message is this station, calling everyone in the city… right here. To you.
And the voice on the tape? The name tag on the recorder says… Vickers.

## Final
So, here's where we are. A dog. Fifteen bullets. Fifty-one gallons of water. Every light in the building on… and the only officer who knows the way out is standing at the glass, knocking.
Zombies took the city at nine… and you're still locked inside the old police station.
Do you open the door? Comment yes or no… most comments decide Day two.
