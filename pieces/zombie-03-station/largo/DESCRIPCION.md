## Descripción (copiar/pegar)

Zombies took the city at nine, and you're locked inside the old police station. One night, hour by hour: the glass doors, the armory, water and food math, a K-9 named Ribbon, a survivor behind the records door, a clover key… and a radio that keeps repeating the same message. Same words. Same cough.

Do you open the door? Comment YES or NO — most comments decide Day 2.

Real survival facts in this video: FEMA (1 gallon of water per person per day), CDC (never run a generator indoors; 24 h awake ≈ 0.10% BAC), FCC (texts get through when calls can't), the rule of threes, tempered glass, 9 mm ≈ 160 dB.
