# Why New Dogs Instantly Hate Each Other (zaw-dog #6)

EN, about 38 s, ~125 words, deadpan narrator, one cut per line (14 cuts). Dog version of Zaw's "Why New Cats Instantly Hate Each Other" (10M, 2026-11-12). PawPoly (17 shorts, checked 2026-11-12) has no video with this title or topic; the closest is "the doggy handshake" (2.7K, butt sniffing only). Twist: it's not the other dog, it's the leash and you.

**Sources (real):**
- Wilson, Campbell et al. 2022, *PLOS ONE* (Queen's University Belfast): dogs told breath + sweat samples taken from people under stress from the same people relaxed, 93.75 % of the time.
- Sundman et al. 2019, *Scientific Reports* (Linköping University, Sweden): hair cortisol of dogs and their owners; long-term stress levels synchronise, the owner's driving the dog's.
- Greeting etiquette (canine ethology, standard): a curved approach + side / rear sniff is polite; a straight, staring approach is a challenge; a short tight leash forces the straight approach.

**Loop:** line 14 runs into line 1 ("…because otherwise / two dogs meet on a walk…"). **Hero bark:** in the gap after "five seconds later…". **Cast:** the approved v2 dog, the approved owner, a NEW second dog (another breed, same rig, Flow photos), and the dog walker (Mac cast pack) holding it.
