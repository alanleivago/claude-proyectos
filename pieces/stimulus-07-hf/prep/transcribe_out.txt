Youth club directors and coaches, watch this first before ordering uniforms for next season. There are five things you need, and if you skip one, trust me, you'll feel it. Number one, sign off on the final design. Don't guess that the uniform company knows you like it. Ask to see the final design file. Check your colors, patterns, logos, and your sponsors. Then sign off on it. Number two, a real printed sample. Here's the thing. Your screen shows one thing, but fabric shows the truth. Hold it in your hands before you say yes. Number three, sizing samples. Every brand fits a little differently, so let your players try them on at practice. Number four, have a hard delivery date. Make sure it's clear when those uniforms have to be in your hands no matter what. Start 12 weeks before that date? Perfect. Start four weeks before? That's a panic. And number five, extras. New players show up, jerseys get lost, it happens. Grab two or three spare sets per team to eliminate any hassle. Get those five right, and uniform day feels like a Christmas morning, not a return request. And hey, at Stimulus, the design and the printed sample are free. No deposit. Start a design request.
