# Inunaki — brief

## Spec
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 71 s (1704 frames), English first-person paragraphs (`ykPara`), no voice. Style **yokai** (kit unchanged: 216×384, toon 3 bands, teal palette, warm 2×2 dot screen, rain/fog, no vignette).
- Japanese urban legend (Inunaki Village / the old Inunaki tunnel, Fukuoka), retold as fiction: my brother's eighteenth birthday.
- Card: Flow / Nano Banana Pro print (earlobe card prints as style refs), 3 generations, kept #1 (three bandaged one-eyed villagers with sickles at a tunnel mouth, lanterns, a howling dog) → `assets/print.jpg`, `ykCard` pw 90. Badge `ykBadge` (kodama.jpg) centred top from 66.2 s.

## World
One three.js set: the old road cut into the mountain (cedars, guard rails, curve mirror, delineators, puddles, debris, a fallen barricade, 通行止 sign), the masonry portal (voussoirs, plaque 犬鳴隧道, parapet, ivy, moss) sealed with graffitied concrete blocks, the hand-painted sign (この先、日本国憲法 通用せず / BEYOND THIS POINT, THE LAW OF JAPAN DOES NOT APPLY, system Hiragino fonts on a canvas texture), an 80 m lined tunnel, and beyond its far mouth the drowned village (thatched houses, lanterns with reflections in flooded fields, jizō, torii, reeds, howling dogs). A white 1990s RHD sedan (headlight beams, plates 福岡). My brother (sukajan, jeans, high-tops, red flashlight). Eleven villagers (2.8 m, long arms, ragged noragi with patches, wrapped lumpy heads, one bloodshot eye, loose bandage ends, waraji, sickles).

## Claims (from the brief, web-checked by the caller)
- Inunaki Village is a 1990s Japanese urban legend tied to the Inunaki pass, Fukuoka (Wikipedia "Inunaki Village").
- Legend: a hand-written sign at the entrance says the Japanese constitution/law does not apply beyond (same source).
- The old Inunaki tunnel is sealed with concrete blocks (same source).
- The village is said to lie under the Inunaki Dam, completed 1994 (same source; dam completion year).
