## Claims + sources
- Usain Bolt 44,72 km/h — peak speed in the 100 m world record, Berlin 2009 (widely reported; no primary timing report found in the search).
- Speed of sound ≈ 1.235 km/h (343 m/s, air at 20 °C, sea level).
- SR-71A (caption "Más rápido que un SR-71."): 3.529,56 km/h, 28 July 1976 (Guinness World Records; FAI record file 8879).
- Orbital ≈ 28.000 km/h (7,8 km/s LEO); escape 40.270 km/h (11,2 km/s).
- Parker Solar Probe 692.000 km/h (430,000 mph), 24 Dec 2024 (NASA). 692.000 / 1.079.252.849 km/h = 0,064 %.
- Relativity on screen is computed, not illustrative: aberration cos θ' = (cos θ + β)/(1 + β cos θ); Doppler D = 1/(γ(1 − β cos θ')); "1 s tuyo = γ s en la Tierra".
