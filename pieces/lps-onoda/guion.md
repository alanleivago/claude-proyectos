# The Soldier Who Fought For 29 Extra Years (punkpolyshorts #2)

EN, ~40 s, about 135 words. Kaneda_punk plays Hiroo Onoda. The last line runs back into the first one, so the video loops.

| # | Voice | On screen | Kaneda expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | This man kept fighting World War Two for 29 years after it ended. | PS1 jungle at night. Kaneda pops out of the ferns, rifle up. Big "29" in 3D. | stern |
| 2 | In 1944, Japan sent him to a tiny island in the Philippines with one order: | Map of Lubang, a cartoon plane, the uniform brand new (wear 0). | neutral |
| 3 | never surrender. | His commander barks the order; punch-in on the star on the cap. | angry |
| 4 | A year later, the war ended. Nobody told him. | City celebrating with confetti → hard cut back to Kaneda in the bushes. | blank |
| 5 | Well, they tried. Planes dropped leaflets saying it was over. | Leaflets rain down; one lands on his face. | skeptic |
| 6 | He decided they were fake. | He crumples it up and tosses it away. | stern |
| 7 | So he kept going. One year. Ten. Twenty. | Fast montage: the uniform fades and fills with patches (wear 1 → 2) and a calendar flips. | tired → nervous |
| 8 | His three comrades left, one by one. | Three soldiers in other colours (green, red, purple) vanish from the camp one at a time. | sad |
| 9 | In 1974, a college dropout went into that jungle looking for three things: a panda, the Yeti, and Lieutenant Onoda. | Backpacker with a checklist; panda and Yeti cameos as wall easter eggs. | — |
| 10 | He found one of them. | Face to face in the ferns. Onoda's rifle at his nose. | shocked |
| 11 | Onoda still wouldn't stop, unless his old commander ordered him to. | Crossed arms, deadpan. | skeptic |
| 12 | So Japan found that commander, who was selling books by then, and flew him to the island. | Old man behind a bookshop counter → on the plane. | — |
| 13 | He read the order out loud. Onoda saluted, | The old man unrolls the paper; Onoda salutes, the uniform in tatters. | sad → happy (one tear) |
| 14 | and went home, after 29 years of fighting a war that was already over. | He walks off; the uniform turns back into the blue jacket. Loop → "This man kept fighting…" | neutral |

**Cast (with the new rule of more characters):**
- Onoda: Kaneda with the cap and havelock, in 3 wear stages.
- The commander: old man in uniform, later as a bookseller.
- 3 comrades: Kaneda recolours.
- The student Suzuki: backpack and glasses.
- Celebrating crowd.
- Plane.
- Cameos: panda and Yeti.

**Facts (checked):**
- Onoda landed on Lubang in December 1944 under orders not to surrender.
- He rejected the leaflets as propaganda.
- His comrades: Akatsu surrendered in 1950; Shimada died in 1954; Kozuka died in 1972.
- Norio Suzuki found him in February 1974. He was looking for "Lieutenant Onoda, a panda and the Abominable Snowman".
- Major Taniguchi, by then a bookseller, relieved him of duty on 9 March 1974.

The script says "the Yeti", true to Suzuki's "Abominable Snowman".
