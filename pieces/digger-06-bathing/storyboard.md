# Digger 06: storyboard v1

`storyboard_v1.png`: 16 shots on provisional timing (no voice yet), ~73 s. Style frames: `review/styleframes_v1.png` (shots 3, 5, 8, 13, 15 full size). Engine = the vertical Digger 05 engine (Exp01 sets) + `engine/d06_shots.js`.

**Nothing flat:**
- **CLEAN meter:** a physical MML2 HUD panel parented to the camera, with a bevelled dark plate, teal rim, extruded "CLEAN" and 5 emissive cells. Steps: river 1, strigil 2 (stews: cell 3 flickers and dies at "down"), dry wash 3, Feetham 4, shower 5.
- **Labels and years:** extruded jittered 3D letters (#FFE21A / amber, no outline) popping in, tilted, in perspective.
- **Eras:** sepia is the scene's own material/light grade plus a warm light, not a 2D layer.
