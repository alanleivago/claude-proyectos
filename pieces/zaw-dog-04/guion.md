# Your Dog Thinks You're Cheating When You Pet Another Dog (zaw-dog #4)

EN, about 37 s, ~125 words, deadpan narrator, one cut per sentence (14 cuts). It follows Zaw's "Your Cat Thinks You're a Traitor" formula. PawPoly hasn't covered this topic.

**Loop:** line 14 runs into line 1 ("…the second you / pet another dog,"). **Hero bark:** in the comma gap after "dog," in line 1.

**Cast:**
- **Our dog:** the approved v2.
- **The owner:** the approved owner.
- **The rival:** a stuffed "fake dog" prop in a different breed and colour that barks and wags. In the park shots (lines 1 and 14) it's a real dog, a recoloured and resized v2 so it reads as a different breed.

## Fact check (2026-11-08)
- **Harris & Prouvost 2014,** "Jealousy in Dogs", *PLoS ONE* 9(7):e94597, UC San Diego (lines 3-10):
  - 36 dogs, tested at home.
  - The owners ignored their dog and played with an animated stuffed dog, a jack-o'-lantern pail or a pop-up book.
  - The dogs pushed or touched the owner 78% of the time with the fake dog and 42% with the pumpkin.
  - About 30% got between the owner and the fake dog.
  - A quarter snapped at the fake dog, against one dog with the pumpkin and the book.
  - 86% sniffed the fake dog's rear.
  - The authors say they can't be sure it's jealousy as people feel it; "thinks you're cheating" is the deadpan title frame.
- **Bastos et al. 2021,** "Dogs Mentally Represent Jealousy-Inducing Social Interactions", *Psychological Science* 32(5), University of Auckland (lines 11-13):
  - 18 dogs on a leash; the owner stroked a realistic fake dog behind a screen.
  - The dogs pulled hard, with force similar to when they could see it.
  - With a fleece cylinder, they pulled far less.
