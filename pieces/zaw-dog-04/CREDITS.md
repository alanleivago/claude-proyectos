## Images (Google Flow, Nano Banana Pro, one image per call)
- A 3x3 dog-park sheet: picket fence, bench (front, side), poop-bag dispenser with bin, "DOG PARK" sign, park tree, bush, bin, fountain. This is the new DOG PARK set (`prep_04.py`), which joins the kit.
- A 3x3 props sheet: jack-o'-lantern pail, pop-up book, three-panel room divider, fleece bolster, red leash, swatches.
- A plush toy German Shepherd sheet (side, front, back): the fake dog, built from its side photo with heightfield inflation.
- A loose-fist hand sheet (the "handle" hand pose gripping the pail in 05a).
- Reused: the owner (zaw-dog #3), the "pointer" person (zaw-dog #2) as the leash assistant, the kit's living room and hallway, the hand and shoe sheets, the funny dog pictures and the "DOGGO POLY SHORTS" tag.
- The dog: the approved zaw-dog v2 model (`pieces/zaw-dog-v2`). The rival is the same model with a recoloured white coat (`prep_04.py`), 0.82x the size, with a smoothed, displaced fluffy coat.
