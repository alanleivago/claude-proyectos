# ciudades — ¿Qué pasaría con las ciudades si todos los humanos desaparecieran?

## Spec
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 70 s. Spanish, metric. Style **pov3d** (three.js r149), new world. Signed ALTERBASETV.
- From the reference `Ref lunar animation/What would happen to our cities if every human disappeared tomorrow?.mp4`: a fixed POV over a city that empties and is reclaimed, with a time-since-humans counter.
- Ours: a floating camera (handheld sums of sines, 96 m up) over a brick avenue that runs to a cable-stayed bridge across a river; a glass skyline on the far bank, an elevated metro, a Ferris wheel, palms, lamps, billboards, ~70 cars and ~520 people. People vanish at 4 s; cars crash; the lights go out; dogs, leaves, weeds, grass, deer; rust; the Ferris wheel (40) and the metro viaduct (43) fall; the bridge collapses (45.2); the towers fall (48–54); a forest; an ice age; a million years.

## Claims + sources
- World population ~8.2 billion (2025) — UN World Population Prospects 2024.
- Power stations shut down within hours to days without operators; city lights go out; drains clog, flooding; weeds crack paving; steel rusts; bridges fall in roughly centuries; skyscrapers fall over centuries; glass, plastics and the Apollo footprints on the Moon outlast us — Alan Weisman, *The World Without Us* (2007), and *Life After People* (History, 2008); the reference video makes the same claims.
- Illustrative, not exact: the time scale between beats, the order of the falls, the ice age at "8.590 años" as the counter runs (a glaciation within ~50,000 years is a model estimate, not a fixed date).
