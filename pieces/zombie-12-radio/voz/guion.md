# Ep12 "AVA: The Radio" (guion humanizado, 2a persona)

The lights are out, and your phone has no bars… When the phones die, this is your best friend.
A hand-crank or battery radio doesn't need the power grid or a cell tower… Crank it for a minute, and it keeps talking for a while.
Plug in one earbud and keep the speaker off, because noise brings them… Your other ear stays on the house.
At night, AM stations bounce off the sky and carry for hundreds of miles… So scan the dial after dark.
Save your batteries and listen at the top of the hour, when the news comes on… Then write down every time and frequency.
Listen for what keeps repeating, like shelters, safe routes, and curfew times… If you hear it every hour, it's probably official.
But a stranger inviting you somewhere isn't a rescue… Listening is free. Talking back tells them where you are.
