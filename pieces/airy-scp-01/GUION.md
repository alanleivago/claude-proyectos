# Airy SCP ep. 1: "Who blinks first" (SCP-173)

Voces: Airy = "Voz ashley clean limpia" 60ea9b99 (rápida, coqueta) · Guardia = "Guardia scp" 8bd4e6c7 (lento, deadpan).
Subtítulos: rosa claro = Airy, amarillo = guardia. Sala: assets/scp-rooms/scp173-chamber. ~220 palabras, 61–63 s. Ritmo passthesalt: réplicas de 1–3 s pegadas (gap 0,1–0,25 s), voz a ~3,6 palabras/s; solo hay silencio en la fila 23 (~1 s) y la 32 (2 s).

| # | Quién | Línea | Pose / plano |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AIRY | Hi, handsome. Do you ever... blink? | Apoyada en la pared con la cadera, cabeza ladeada hacia el 173 (en cuadro). Wide de establecimiento. |
| 2 | GUARD | Step away from the statue. Slowly. | Guardia entra a cuadro, mano levantada. |
| 3 | AIRY | Relax, we're just talking. He's the strong, silent type. | Se enrosca un mechón en el dedo. |
| 4 | GUARD | He's the strong, silent, snap-your-neck type. | Contraplano guardia, OTS de Airy desenfocada. |
| 5 | AIRY | Rude. Look at those cheekbones. | Mano en la mejilla, mirada soñadora al 173. |
| 6 | GUARD | That's SCP-173. | Insert placa: SCP-173 · EUCLID. |
| 7 | AIRY | Is that his number? | Saca el celular, sonrisa pícara. |
| 8 | GUARD | It moves when nobody's looking at it. | Contrapicado del 173. |
| 9 | AIRY | Aww, so he's shy. | Manos juntas en el pecho. |
| 10 | GUARD | One blink. That's all he needs to cross this room and break your neck. | Push-in lento al guardia. |
| 11 | AIRY | What happened to the last girl who stared at him? | Se muerde el labio, curiosa. |
| 12 | GUARD | Which part of her? | Deadpan, sin girar la cabeza. |
| 13 | AIRY | Okay, but he'd have to make the first move. | Hombro hacia adelante, guiño. |
| 14 | GUARD | That... is literally the problem. | Guardia se pasa la mano por el visor. |
| 15 | GUARD | And every week somebody mops his cell. Nobody knows what that red stuff is. | Insert: manchas en el piso de la celda. |
| 16 | AIRY | A man who leaves a mess. Red flag. | Cruza los brazos, ceja levantada. |
| 17 | AIRY | Fine. I'll stare at him all night. I'm amazing at eye contact. | Pestañea lento hacia el guardia (sin mirar al 173). |
| 18 | GUARD | Then why are you holding eye drops? | Insert: gotero en su mano. |
| 19 | AIRY | My eyes get dry when I flirt. | Encoge los hombros, sonrisa. |
| 20 | GUARD | Wait, wait, wait. You put those in. In here. | Guardia gira la cabeza de golpe. |
| 21 | AIRY | Only one eye! The other one was watching him. | Se tapa un ojo con la mano, pose "peekaboo". |
| 22 | GUARD | ...Which one? | Primer plano guardia. |
| 23 | AIRY | ...Oh. | Silencio, sin subtítulo. Plano del 173: un poco más cerca que antes. |
| 24 | GUARD | Protocol is three people in here. You blink, you say it out loud. | Guardia fija la vista en el 173. |
| 25 | AIRY | Three? Two is way more romantic. | Se acerca al guardia, dedo en su pechera. |
| 26 | GUARD | Don't. | Deadpan. |
| 27 | AIRY | Is that a no, or a not-in-front-of-him? | Mira de reojo al 173, mano en la cadera. |
| 28 | GUARD | It's a no. | |
| 29 | AIRY | Your eyes are kinda pretty, by the way. | Se para en puntas de pie, cara cerca de su visor. |
| 30 | GUARD | You can't even see my eyes. | |
| 31 | AIRY | I can tell. | Sonrisa, guiño. |
| 32 | — | (2 s de silencio: los dos se miran. El 173, desenfocado al fondo, ya está detrás de ellos.) | Two-shot, 173 en el fondo con DOF. |
| 33 | GUARD | ...Neither of us is looking at him, are we? | Corte a negro + crujido de piedra. Fin seco. |
