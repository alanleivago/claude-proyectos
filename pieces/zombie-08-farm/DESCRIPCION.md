**Título:** Zombie Outbreak: Day 1 Alone on a Farm
**Miniatura:** `miniatura_1280x720.png`

Day one of a zombie outbreak, and you're on a farm forty miles from town. You've got a well, a barn, twelve cows, a loud bunch of chickens and one very good dog. Here's how you get through the first day, from water and noise to the gate… and what's waiting in the barn. Part 2 is coming.

Facts used:
- Ready.gov: 1 gallon of water per person per day.
- CPSC: run generators outdoors only, at least 20 ft from doors, windows and vents.
- FoodSafety.gov: a full freezer stays cold for about 48 hours if you keep it closed.
- Extension services: a cow drinks about 10 to 20 gallons a day.
