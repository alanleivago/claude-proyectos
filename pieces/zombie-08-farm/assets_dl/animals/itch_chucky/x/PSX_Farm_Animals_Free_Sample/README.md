# PSX Farm Animals – Free Sample (Chucky Assets)

1 animated model: a white chicken with 5 baked loops inside the GLB: walk, run, idle, peck and flap. Rigid PS1-style parts, no skinning needed; set the loop mode on import.

These are the same models, textures and quality as the full pack. Use them in your game, free (licence in LICENSE.txt).

## Want the rest?
**PSX Farm Animals** — 10 animated animals (cow, horse, pig, sheep, chicken in 2 coats each, 6 clips per quadruped) plus a wander-AI script and a meadow demo with pens.
https://chuckyassets.itch.io/psx-farm-animals
