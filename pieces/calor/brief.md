## Claims + sources
- Asphalt (bitumen) has no melting point; it softens gradually, softening points from ~42–49 °C upward by grade — ASTM D3461; bitumen tables (PMC9201880).
- Water boils at 100 °C (sea level).
- HDPE (plastic) melts ~120–135 °C — material guides (rapid-protos, plasticranger).
- Popcorn pops at ~180 °C — Science News Explores; Journal of Cereal Science (1983: ~177 °C).
- Paper autoignition ~233 °C (451 °F; range 218–246 °C) — Wikipedia "Autoignition temperature".
- Petrol autoignition ~247–280 °C — Wikipedia "Autoignition temperature".
- Wood ignition ~300 °C (lower bound; species/moisture) — University of Cambridge safety table (hsd073c).
- Venus surface ~464–465 °C — NASA ("Why is Venus so hot?"); space centre FAQs.
- Draper point: solids start to glow dull red at ~525 °C (798 K).
- Structural steel loses ~40 % of its strength at 550 °C, the usual critical temperature — Rockwool, Promat, Nullifire.
- Illustrative: the steam cloud and virga (steam is invisible; it condenses high up), the boiling front of the sea, the fire timings.
