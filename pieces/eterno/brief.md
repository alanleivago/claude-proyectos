## Spec
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 70 s. Spanish, metric. Style **pov3d** (three.js r149), new world. Signed ALTERBASETV.

## Claims + sources
- ~62.4 million deaths in 2024, ~170,900 a day; ~132.5 million births a year — UN WPP 2024 via Statistics Times (statisticstimes.com/demographics/world-death-and-birth-rate.php).
- 18 billion by 2100: 8.2 billion + ~132 million/yr × 75 yr with no deaths (illustrative arithmetic).
- *Turritopsis dohrnii* can revert from a mature medusa to a polyp (transdifferentiation) under stress, injury or age; it can still die of predation or disease — Wikipedia; AMNH ("The Immortal Jellyfish").
- Jeanne Calment, 21 Feb 1875 – 4 Aug 1997, 122 years 164 days, the oldest verified person — Wikipedia; Gerontology Research Group.
- Light pollution hides the Milky Way from city skies (general).
- Illustrative: the clock (years, population curve), the towers' distances, the order of growth.
