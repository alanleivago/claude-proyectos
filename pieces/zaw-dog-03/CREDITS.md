## Images (Google Flow, Nano Banana, one image per call)
- The stranger's character sheet (front, profile, back), built into 3D with `photohuman2.py`.
- A hand clutching a crumpled tissue (the "tissue" hand pose for both people).
- A 3x3 product sheet: mustard armchair (front, side), tissue box, bed (foot end, side), headboard, bedside table with lamp, magnet, slippers.
- A clear acrylic door panel on black (the door's reflections).
- 18 dog portraits (line 3), four funny dog pictures (bedroom, living room), a night sky.
- Two single-shoe sheets (the owner's white sneaker, the stranger's tan Chelsea boot).
- Reused from zaw-dog #1/#2: the owner, the living room, the hallway, the street, the hand sheets, the set textures.
- The TV cartoon (a dog waving at a train) was modelled and rendered in-house (`tools/tv_insert.py`).
- The dog: the approved zaw-dog v2 model (`pieces/zaw-dog-v2`).

## Voice
- Narration: VoiceStudio, the "3d cartoon" profile (227af074), tempo 1.1, retro EQ.

## Research
- Custance & Mayer (2012), "Empathic-like responding by domestic dogs (*Canis familiaris*) to distress in humans: an exploratory study", *Animal Cognition* 15(5):851–859.
- Sanford, Burt & Meyers-Manor (2018), "Timmy's in the well: Empathy and prosocial helping in dogs", *Learning & Behavior*, doi 10.3758/s13420-018-0332-3.
