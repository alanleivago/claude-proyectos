# ¿Qué pasaría si los protones desaparecieran durante 5 segundos?

- **Spec:** 75 s, 1080×1920, 24 fps, pov3d (three.js PBR, bloom, vignette, grain, motion blur on camera turns). Spanish.
- **Reference:** "What if protons suddenly disappeared for 5 seconds" (Ref Lunar Animation), with the same shots and camera moves. The place is changed to a park lake with a promenade and towers behind; the people, dog and boats are our own.
- **Focus:** detailed first-person hands.
  - Seamless tube fingers, nails with lunulae, palm creases, pads, a bare forearm with the wrist bump.
  - In the magenta phase the hands turn glassy, with bones, vessels and voronoi cracks.

## Claims + sources
- **"Cada átomo tiene al menos un protón" / "los protones deciden qué elemento es cada átomo":** the atomic number is the proton count (IUPAC definition of atomic number).
- **"Unos 10⁵⁰ átomos en la Tierra":** about 1.3×10⁵⁰ (Earth mass 5.97×10²⁴ kg; standard estimate, e.g. Fermilab "How many atoms are in the Earth?").
- **"Los protones son cerca de la mitad de la masa de tu cuerpo":**
  - Body mass by element is O 65 %, C 18 %, H 10 %, N 3 %. Hydrogen is all proton; O, C and N are half protons. That puts protons at about 54 % of body mass.
  - Changed from "casi" to "cerca de", because the share is slightly more than half.
