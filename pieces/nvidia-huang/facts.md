## Still uncertain
- Exact stock-drop percentages (outlets differ by time of quote); no reliable dollar figure.
- Whether Alice Huang actually was an MSS agent (allegation; China denies; no charges). Even Trump said it is "not yet clear".
- Status/scope of the FBI inquiry (only Trump's remark; FBI no comment).
- Huang has never publicly named Alice Huang as the woman in his email; the link comes from news reports.
- Why the board accepted the resignation (filing gives no reason; WSJ reported an ouster was being prepared).
