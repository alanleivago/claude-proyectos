## Fact check (2026-11-03)

- **Date:** confirmed. The Hoftag was called for 25 July 1184 (the feast of Saint James), and the floor collapsed on 26 July. The script says "July 1184".
- **Henry VI:** confirmed. He was the son of Frederick Barbarossa and already King of the Romans. Barbarossa sent him to mediate, turning him aside from a campaign toward Poland. He was born in November 1165, so he was 18. In the script he is "Henry, eighteen".
- **The dispute:** confirmed. It was a land dispute between Archbishop Conrad of Mainz (Konrad von Wittelsbach) and Landgrave Louis III of Thuringia.
- **Building:** this is **uncertain**. Wikipedia says it was a two-story building near Erfurt Cathedral and that sources disagree between the provost's building and the bishop's residence. Popular sites say "the Petersberg" (citadel or St. Peter's monastery). The script names no building.
- **Collapse:** confirmed. The rotten wooden beams of the upper floor gave way, the ground floor went down too, and people fell into the cesspit underneath.
- **Death toll:** about 60 according to Wikipedia and most sources. Some popular sites say 60–100. The script says "sixty" and "about sixty". The victims were nobles and their retinues; the named ones are counts (Abenberg, Schwarzburg, Ziegenhain, Kirchberg, Wartburg). "Nobles" is the standard framing.
- **Survival:** Henry and Conrad sat in a stone window niche or alcove and held on until rescuers brought ladders. Wikipedia does not mention **iron bars**; some popular accounts do. The bars only appear on screen and are not in the narration.
- **Louis III:** Wikipedia says he fell but survived. Some popular sites say only that he survived, without saying how. The line "fell in too. He lived." rests on Wikipedia and the Reinhardsbrunn chronicle, so it is **slightly uncertain**.
- **Primary sources** (per Wikipedia): *Cronica Reinhardsbrunnensis* (MGH SS XXX/1, 541–542) and *Cronica S. Petri Erfordensis moderna* (MGH SS XXX/1, 374).

## Humanizer pass

The narration has no not-X-but-Y contrasts, no dashes and no staged closers. The facts list (beams, floors, pit) is kept short and in plain past tense. The only hook-style line is line 1, which is the format. Contractions are not needed in such short lines. Nothing was added beyond the sources.
