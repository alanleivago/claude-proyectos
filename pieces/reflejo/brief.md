# Brief: ¿Qué pasaría si tu reflejo dejara de imitarte?

## The idea in one sentence

The reflection falls out of step one small thing at a time (late, no blink, a smile), and the last line turns it on the viewer: "…pero tú no."

## Format and spec

- Format: beat-cut (held shots with hard cuts on the 8th grid and a held payoff)
- Size: 1080x1920, 24fps, 120 BPM grid, 15.0s = 360 frames
- Style: `onebit` (new, 1-bit manga-horror pixel), 180x320 internal (6 px), 4-step grey ramp

## Beats

| # | t (s) | what happens | cut | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 | over the shoulder: the reflection, centred close-up, slow push-in | hard | room tone, tube hum, drip, type clicks |
| 2 | 3-6 | the hand rises; the reflection's follows 0.85 s late | hard | cloth rustle, then a softer reverbed one |
| 3a | 6-7.5 | the person, seen from the mirror, blinks | hard | hum, drip |
| 3b | 7.5-9 | the reflection, same framing, doesn't blink and stares | hard | hum |
| 4 | 9-12.5 | medium shot: the person steps back, the reflection presses its palms on the glass and grins; the tube dies at 11.9 | hard on the hit | step, glass creaks, flicker, click; silence 11.9-12.5 |
| 5 | 12.5-15 | extreme close-up of the grin, the glass cracks | end | sub + distorted impact + glass crack (loudest), ringing tail |

## Sound plan

No music. Room tone, 100 Hz tube buzz, very low air drone, drips, per-glyph clicks, rustle, creaks; everything cuts dead at 11.9 s (0.5 s of silence), the sting on the 12.5 s cut, then a dark low bed and an inharmonic glass ring.
