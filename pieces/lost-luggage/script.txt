You land, you wait at the carousel, and your suitcase never comes out. The airline searches for months, then gives up. So where does it go? Alabama. There's a store in a small town there that buys lost luggage nobody ever claimed, opens it, and sells everything inside. You walk in, and it's like a mall built out of other people's vacations. Shirts, cameras, headphones, wedding dresses, a whole rack of ski boots… in Alabama. Then a guy at the register holds up a jacket. "Hey, this looks just like mine." He checks the pocket. "It is mine." You see, this place is called Unclaimed Baggage, and it's been doing this since 1970. Airlines find almost every bag they lose. But the few that stay lost for about three months get sold, and this store buys them without knowing what's inside. Workers open thousands of new items every single day. Most of it gets washed, priced and put on a shelf. Some gets donated. And some is just strange. They've found a suit of armor, a live rattlesnake, and even an ancient Egyptian burial mask. So next time your suitcase goes missing, don't panic. It's probably fine. It's just in Alabama… on sale.
