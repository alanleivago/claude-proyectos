## Sound effects and ambience

Real recordings from Freesound.org and Wikimedia Commons, trimmed, layered, level-adjusted, equalised, panned and pitch-shifted by resampling for the film. On top of the recordings sits a light code-synthesized layer made for this film (tools/synth.py, seeded: the teaser rise, a sub under the big red-counter jumps, the space drone, the slow-motion coin shimmer and a tiny ping on the coin's edge); it uses no third-party audio and needs no credit.
