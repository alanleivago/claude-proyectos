# Ep5 "Ava: the ghost behind you (P.T.)" (guion humanizado)

Did you know that in P.T.… a ghost walks right behind you the whole time?
And nobody noticed for five years.
The whole game is one hallway. You walk out the door at the end… and you're right back at the start, and every loop gets a little weirder.
You'd hear breathing behind you… you'd turn around… and nothing.
Then a modder hacked the camera… so he could finally see behind the player.
And there she was. Lisa, stuck to your back from the moment you pick up the flashlight, following you step by step.
Every time you turned around… the game would hide her. Face forward again… and she's back.
So Ava tried a little trick. If you can't turn around… use the front camera. She held up the phone… and there was a second face on the screen.
Then she felt cold breath right on her neck.
Oh, fudge. (toma del ep1)
So that feeling of someone right behind you? It was real.
Send this to the friend who plays at night… and never looks back.
