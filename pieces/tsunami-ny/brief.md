## Spec
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 70 s (1680 frames). Spanish; title in the user's chosen words. Style **pov3d** (three.js r149), new world.
- Format **F5, fixed place**: a rooftop in lower Manhattan (west side, 92 m up) at sunset, looking south over downtown and the harbour.
