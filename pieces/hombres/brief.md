# hombres — ¿Qué pasaría si todos los hombres de la Tierra desaparecieran?

- **Format:** chronology told through one family (Lucía, 8, and her mother Ana), 9:16, 180 s, 24 fps, pov3d (three.js, PBR + bloom). Spanish.
- **Reference:** `Ref Lunar Animation/What if every Man on Earth disappeared.mp4` (210 s). Kept: the arena countdown 6→0, the world-population HUD, the bike → same street, same bike arc, the end card text with the share arrow. Shortened to 3:00 and focused on the faces: heroes with eyes that look and blink, lids, brows, a mouth that smiles/opens/trembles, tears, cheeks, hands.
- **Sound:** CC0 recordings per scene (arena, suburb, cockpit, vigil, trench, hall, hospital, supermarket, classroom, night crickets, spark, city, truck, building site, lab, clinic, newborn, children) + code SFX (scoreboard beeps 6→1, THE BUZZER at 10.0 with the vanishing shimmer and a sub drop, father's shimmer, master caution, power down/up, fetal heartbeat, end tone). No music.
