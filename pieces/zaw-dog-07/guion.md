# Your Dog Knows When You're Lying (zaw-dog #7)

EN, 16.2 s, 48 words, deadpan narrator, 14 cuts (1.16 s average). This is the first video in the new Zaw structure: 5 beats, no loop and no stacks of numbers. The VoiceStudio "3d cartoon" voice runs at TEMPO 1.1 and then through retro_eq.

**Hero bark:** in a 0.5 s gap after "knows" (0.99-1.49 s). **Cast (kit v6, as built):** the owner (the "liar") and the mailman (the stranger, `styles/zawdog/humans/mailman`, .blend unchanged). **Sets (v2):** the kit living room (set x1.35, the #6 final v1 pixel sky in the windows) and the kit hallway + open front door (porch, street photo) for the mailman. **Props:** the kit's two steel bowls, low-poly lofts of Flow photos (full and empty). No new Flow images were needed. **Gasps:** 2 (the owner scared in 04c, surprised in 06b).

**Topic overlap:** #2 used the same Takaoka study (lines 2-8, inside the fake-throw story). Here it carries the whole video, as asked.

## Fact check (2026-11-13)

**Takaoka, Maeda, Hori & Fujita (2015)**, "Do dogs follow behavioral cues from an unreliable human?", *Animal Cognition* 18(2):475-483, doi 10.1007/s10071-014-0816-2, Kyoto University. 34 pet dogs (AKC report).
- Object-choice task: food hidden in one of two containers. Phase 1: most dogs followed the experimenter's pointing (line 2).
- Phase 2: the dogs were shown what was in both containers, then the same experimenter pointed at the EMPTY one (line 3, "pointed at an empty bowl").
- Phase 3, the Phase 1 procedure again: "many fewer" dogs followed that person (line 4, "most dogs ignored", which is the reverse reading of "many fewer followed"; the script never says "all").
- Experiment 2: after Phases 1-2, a NEW experimenter pointed in Phase 3 and the dogs followed (line 5, "a stranger pointed").
- Not in the paper: the mailman (line 6 is a deadpan joke about the new person), real food bowls (the study used containers), and the claim that a single lie is enough (not stated, so the script avoids "once"). The study does not frame it as "lying"; the authors say dogs infer the reliability of the person giving the cue.

## Known weak points (final; API review CASI)
- **Kit-v6 humans:** the hems still show the 1.5 cm texel staircase plus a small white flap on the owner's shoulder. That flap is geometry: torso polygons weighted to the spine fold over the posed arm, so a texture fix can't remove it. The tee collar still has a small notch, and the face oval still reads in 3/4 close-ups (04c, 06b, 06c). The real fix is a kit-level reweight / garment remap of owner and mailman. That is shared with #6 and was NOT done.
- **Hands:** they read as low-poly photo hands. The index now points down at the bowl, but the API still reads 02a and 03b as pointing at the wall.
- **Exterior through the door:** the lawn and sidewalk textures smear at grazing angles (Linear filtering, kept to avoid shimmer). The sidelight glass is flat.
- **People presence:** human-only shots are about 36 %, against the ≤ 20 % rule; the story needs the faces. 04b (0.7 s) and 04a (0.93 s) are short because the voice timing fixes the cuts. "pointed" falls between caption blocks.
- **Unchanged approved assets:** the kit dog's chest patch and grey legs, the TV cabinet's dark base, and the pink lamp-shade rim.
