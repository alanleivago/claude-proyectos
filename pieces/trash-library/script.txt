In Ankara, Turkey, one of the best libraries in town was built out of trash. Imagine you're a garbage collector. It's six in the morning, you're grabbing bags off the sidewalk, and one of them is weirdly heavy. You open it. Books. Good ones. Novels, comics, a whole encyclopedia. Somebody just threw them away. So you put them aside. Then your coworker finds more. And more. Soon the break room is stacked to the ceiling, and your boss walks in and says, "Guys… is this a dump, or a library?" You see, that's exactly what happened. Garbage collectors in Ankara started saving the books people threw out, and they piled up so fast the city turned an old brick factory into a real library. Shelves, reading tables, a kids' corner. All of it filled with books somebody decided they didn't need. It started with a few thousand and grew to tens of thousands. At first it was only for the workers and their families. But it got so big they started lending books to schools, villages, and even prisons all over the country. So next time you throw away an old book… it might not be the end of the story. In Ankara, it might just be chapter two.
