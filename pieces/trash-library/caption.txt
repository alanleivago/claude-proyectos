Garbage Men Built a Library Out of Your Trash 📚

In Ankara, Turkey, sanitation workers kept finding perfectly good books in the garbage, so they started saving them. The pile got so big the city turned an old brick factory into a real library, and now it lends books to schools, villages and even prisons. 🤯

Would you donate your old books, or keep every single one? 👇
