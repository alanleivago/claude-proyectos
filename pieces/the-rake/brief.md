# The Rake: brief

## Spec
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 62 s (1488 frames), English first-person paragraphs (`ykPara`), no voice. Style **yokai**.
- Idea from theredarchivesyt's "The Rake" (151 k views). Retold in Japan as our own story: a mountain minka in winter, the mother-in-law by the irori, the baby in an ejiko cradle. No frames or wording copied.
- Card: Flow / Nano Banana Pro in a new project. Style ref: gashadokuro's Flow print, because `references/earlobe/` is not on this PC. Kept the first generation (it matches the design) → `assets/print.jpg`, border cropped. Seal 爪, with a hand grid added to `YK_SEALG`. Badge from 57.2 s.

## World
- `src/house.js`: the minka (tatami, boards, irori with embers, jizai-kagi, kettle, kamidana with shimenawa, tansu, andon, mino, firewood), the moon-side shōji at x −4.5 (the paper lets the moon through and the lattice throws its grid), the partition to the baby's room at x 3 (panel 2 tears at 34.85), thatch roof, engawa, snow, cedars.
- The Rake has a two-bone IK crawl on all fours. Its claw tips are clamped to the floor or futon (`surfY`), and its knees sit above the hips. It has asymmetric cracked black sockets with a red pin on the left and a white pin on the right, a slit mouth with teeth set in the gum, a shimenawa and shide round its neck, an ofuda on its back, and rust streaks on the skin.
