# encoger — ¿Qué pasaría si no dejaras de encogerte?

## Spec
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 80 s. Spanish, metric. Style **pov3d** (three.js r149, logarithmic depth), new worlds. Signed ALTERBASETV.
- From the reference `Ref lunar animation/What if you kept shrinking.mp4` (98 s, a lawn). Ours: a Valdivian forest floor (coihue trunks with fissured bark and hanging lichen, pinnate ferns, colihue canes, leaf litter, a mossy log with orange mushrooms, light shafts) — the tardigrade's real habitat; then a dewdrop, a tardigrade, a cell, DNA, an atom, its nucleus, quarks. Each step inward is a seamless zoom-dissolve.
- HUD: TU ALTURA (1,75 m → 0,7 mm), then VECES MÁS PEQUEÑO in Spanish long scale (miles, millones, billones, trillones).
- Card: title · NADIE SABE SI EXISTE ALGO MÁS PEQUEÑO. · "Pero tranquilo, esto no puede pasar... ¿o sí?" · ALTERBASETV.

## Claims + sources
- Tardigrades live in moss ("moss piglets"); most are under 0.5 mm — Wikipedia "Tardigrade"; Carolina Biological ("Tardigrades: little water bears").
- Too small for light: visible wavelengths are ~400–700 nm; below a few hundred nanometres light can't resolve detail (general optics).
- The atom is mostly empty: ~10⁻¹⁰ m across vs a nucleus of ~10⁻¹⁵ m (general knowledge; no strong single source found in the search — illustrative).
- Quarks have no measured size; experiments bound their radius below ~10⁻¹⁸ m — H1/ZEUS (arXiv hep-ex/0003002, hep-ex/0305015).
- Nobody knows whether anything is smaller (open question).
- Illustrative: the scale curve, the HUD numbers (rounded), colours of quarks (colour charge is not a visible colour).
