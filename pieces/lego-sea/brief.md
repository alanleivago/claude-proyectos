## Sets
- **beach**: Cornish beach the morning after a storm — wet sand with reflections, wrack line of seaweed, cliffs and a lighthouse, grey sky breaking to gold, tiny Lego pieces in the sand (black dragon, flipper, life jacket), bucket filling up, neighbor + dog.
- **storm** (1997): night ocean, huge rogue wave, container ship rolling, 62 containers sliding off, one bursts open → a cloud of Lego pieces, counter "5,000,000".
- **sea**: underwater — sea-themed pieces drifting (scuba tanks, spear guns, seaweed, octopuses), currents as arrows, pieces sinking/floating, storm pushing them ashore; scientist tracking them on a map.
- **hunt**: people with torches/buckets dragon-hunting after a storm; black dragons common vs a glowing green dragon = lottery ticket gag; final: the one box on the seabed, "refuses to leave".

## Facts (sources in scripts/cartoon3d-guiones-03.md)
13 Feb 1997, Tokio Express hit by a rogue wave off Land's End: 62 containers lost, one with ~4.8 million Lego pieces, many sea-themed (flippers, scuba gear, life jackets, spear guns, seaweed, octopuses, dragons). Pieces still wash up in Cornwall; black dragons found often, green dragons very rare (Tracey Williams, "Lego Lost at Sea"). Oceanographer Curtis Ebbesmeyer studied their drift.
