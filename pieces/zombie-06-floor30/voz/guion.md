# Ep6 "Ava: Floor 30" (guion humanizado)

Imagine you live on the thirtieth floor… and that was the worst thing about your apartment. Until tonight.
Then every phone in the building goes off at once.
First problem: water. The building's pumps run on electricity… so Ava fills the tub, and a bucket, while there's still pressure. A full tub is about forty gallons.
Next, she counts the food… and splits it into days.
Around seven, the city goes dark, block by block… then her building. The fridge dies.
Ava tries the faucet… nothing. Without power, the pumps can't push water up thirty floors.
FEMA says a gallon a day per person… so that tub is about forty days. And her phone's at eighteen percent.
So she pushes the dresser against the door.
Close to midnight, footsteps in the hallway. She freezes… and the footsteps stop too.
Through the peephole, it's the guy from thirty-eleven… staring right at it.
(pantalla: golpes en la puerta + caja de dialogo "PLEASE… I JUST NEED WATER.")
Oh, fudge. (toma del ep1)
So, do you open the door, or stay quiet? Comment one or two… most comments decide Part 2.

Notas:
- Cada "…" = dos tomas separadas (nunca se corta una toma en "…"). Coma = pausa real.
- El vecino no habla con voz: su frase va en una caja de dialogo RE, con los golpes.
- Humanizer: sin listas telegraficas, conectores normales ("so", "then", "and"), sin triadas ni remates de una linea.
