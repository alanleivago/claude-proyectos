# shrink-en — English version of pieces/shrink

Title: "What if you shrank to half your size every 2 seconds?"

Translated (src/overlays.js only): the title, the HUD ("HEIGHT", "HALVES EVERY 2 SECONDS"), all 15 captions and the end card ("IN 60 SECONDS YOU'D BE ABOUT / A BILLION TIMES SMALLER", "But relax, this will never happen…", "… or will it?", ALTERBASE). The html lang is now `en`. The 2 EURO coin texture is unchanged, and the sound was extracted from shrink/renders/final.mp4.

HUD units (fmtH): 12 in and up shows "5 ft 3 in". From 1 to 12 in it shows decimal inches ("7.87 in"). From 1/64 in up to 1 in it shows fractions: 1/16 steps from 1/4 in, 1/32 steps from 1/16 in, and 1/64 steps below that. Under 1/64 in (about 0.016 in, or 397 µm) it switches to µm and then nm.

Review: TEXT PASS (0 cut-off), story arc PASS 6/6, loudness -14.0 LUFS. The end card has a 3 s dead-beat WARN, which is the same as in the Spanish version. Sent to Telegram (42 MB).
