Visual style:
- **Black-figure vase:** only academic clips under 2 min (Panoply, under 140K). Nobody does it long.
- **Bayeux tapestry:** Potion Pictures, 4 min, 1.75M. Deep Down History, 58 min, 952K, on a channel whose median is 4.3K (only 3 videos collected, so that multiple is not reliable).

## Strategy

1. **Positioning:** "Every myth, told in the art of the people who invented it." Greek → amphora. Arthur and Vikings → Bayeux. Egypt → papyrus/tomb. Gilgamesh → Assyrian relief. Dante → engraving.
2. **Format:** "The ENTIRE story of X", 16:9, 25–40 min, divided into chapters.
3. **Flywheel:** each 9:16 short (Scylla, Circe…) is one chapter. The long video is assembled from the chapters at a slower pace, and the shorts get published pointing to it.
4. **Order:**
   1. The Odyssey.
   2. Entire Greek Mythology (in parts).
   3. Gilgamesh.
   4. Dante's Inferno.
   5. King Arthur (Bayeux).
   6. Norse.
   7. Egypt.
5. **Second life:** a "for sleep" version of 2–3 h with the same footage, slower voice and no strong SFX (Bedtime & Historian model).
