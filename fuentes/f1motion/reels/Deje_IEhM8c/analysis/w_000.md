# Shot catalogue, 0.0–20.0 s

Conventions: the frame is 1080×1920 and the sample frames are 384 px wide, so 1 sample px ≈ 2.8 px. At 12 fps, 1 frame ≈ 83 ms. "Line screen" means a fine vertical scanline overlay at ~2–3 px pitch, about 15–25% opacity.

- **New scene type:** Yes. **"Prop stack + ribbon wrap + gold pill"** is a variant of pill+tab but with an isometric object and a ribbon draw-on.
