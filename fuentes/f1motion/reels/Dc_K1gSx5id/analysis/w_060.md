# Shot catalogue, 60.0–80.0 s (frame = 1/12 s; px at 1080×1920; x,y are % of frame)

**Recurring rules in this window**
- **Captions on talking-head and b-roll shots**
  - They sit centered at x 50%, y ≈ 14.8% (about 285 px).
  - Font is a thin/regular lowercase geometric sans (Poppins-like), white, about 40–42 px.
  - They change in 1–3 word chunks on hard cuts. There is no per-word animation, and each chunk holds 0.4–0.9 s.
- **Blur-in landing.** Almost every cut INTO a new shot lands defocused or blurred and sharpens over about 3 frames (0.25 s). Seen at 61.0, 64.5, 66.75 and 71.5.
- **Line screen on b-roll.** All B&W b-roll carries a fine diagonal hatch/halftone line screen at about 45°, with 2–3 px lines. It is absent during the blurred landing frame and fades up with the sharpening.
