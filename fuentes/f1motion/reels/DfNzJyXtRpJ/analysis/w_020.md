# Shot catalogue, 20.0–40.0 s

Frame size is 1080×1920, so 1% of width is 10.8 px and 1% of height is 19.2 px. "On twos" means a pose update every 83 ms at 12 fps. All timings are ±1 frame, because the cut detector fires early on some shots.
