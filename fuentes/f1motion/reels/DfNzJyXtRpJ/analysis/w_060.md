This window is almost entirely live-action and screen-capture. There is no white background, cartoon body, pixel sprite, tab, pill or chevron anywhere in it.

Positions are % of the 1080×1920 frame. Pixel sizes are scaled to 1080 wide.

**Global notes**
- Captions are tiny, white, medium-weight, lowercase-leaning geometric sans (Inter/Helvetica-like).
  - Cap height is about 30 px, so font size is about 44 px, with a faint soft shadow.
  - Centered at x≈50%, y≈12.4%, 1–3 words at a time.
  - They swap with a hard cut, with no pop or slide.

- Live-action footage has a handheld, drifting push-in feel. There is no cartoon "on twos" animation here.
