## S1 · 100.00–102.92 · "Circuit-flag strip list" build (Japan, Miami, Canada, Monaco)
Spoken: "…Japan, Miami, Canada and Monaco."
- **Flag**: this is a **variant of the existing strip list**, not a new type. Each row is a circuit silhouette filled with a country flag instead of a face or vehicle.
- **Background**: flat white #FFFFFF with no texture.
- **Rows** (all identical in structure):
  - **Circuit sprite**
    - Flag-filled track outline with a hard black offset extrude (~3 px down-right).
    - Right edge at x≈43%, width ≈23% (about 250 px).
    - A speed-trail bar of semi-transparent pixel squares in a 4×3 grid runs from the sprite to the left frame edge. The trail has a horizontal gradient from transparent to opaque.
  - **Gold pill**
    - Centre x≈68%, width ≈30% (324 px), height ≈5% (~96 px at 1920).
    - Fill is a gradient #EBD595→#C9A24A with a thin dark outline.
    - Text is wide futuristic caps, black, cap height ≈39 px, centred: "CHINA", "JAPAN", "MIAMI", "CANADA", "MONACO".
  - **Teal chevron tabs** (#6FCDB0 with a white inner stroke): two angled diamond corners flaring from the top-left and top-right of the pill, rotated ±45°.
- **Row colours** (trail gradient, then flag):
  - China: orange #F05A10→yellow #F7C21A, red flag with yellow stars.
  - Japan: pink #F4A8B8→crimson #D8264A, white and red circle.
  - Miami: purple #4A2A6A→magenta #B03060, US flag.
  - Canada: pink→red, maple leaf.
  - Monaco: pink→red, red/white split.
- **Layout**
  - Row pitch ≈12.5% of frame height (~240 px at 1920).
  - China starts at y≈50% (100.0 s).
  - Japan is added at y≈62%, Miami at y≈73%, Canada at ~76%, Monaco at ~77%.
  - After each add, the whole stack eases upward so it stays roughly centred. China ends at y≈27% and Monaco at y≈76% (102.75 s).
- **Animation**
  - New pill: pops in from ~30% scale to 100% in about 3 frames, with a slight overshoot. The tabs flare outward as it grows.
  - New sprite: slides in from the left edge in about 5–6 frames with ease-out. Its trail is sheared and stretched while it moves.
  - The sprite lands about 2 frames after the pill.
  - Each row enters ~0.25 s before its spoken word.
- **Transition out (at 102.92)**: hard cut. The last frame (102.75 s) is slightly softened by compression.
