# Shot list, 140.0–160.0 s (1080x1920, 24 fps)

**Global pattern**
- The reel alternates between A-roll and B-roll.
  - A-roll is a talking-head shot with word-chunk captions.
  - B-roll is a 0.5–2 s archival or news F1 clip, full-bleed, cropped to 9:16.
- Every B-roll clip carries a fine vertical scanline/interlace overlay.
  - The lines are about 2–3 px wide, 50% grey-white, at roughly 15–25% opacity.
  - They fade in over about 6 frames on the first B-roll and stay on all B-roll.
  - They are never on A-roll.
- Every B-roll cut is a hard cut, 0 frames.
  - The only softening is a blur-out on the last 3–4 frames of a shot, or a blur-in on the first 3–4 frames of a shot.
- B-roll cuts land on the spoken beat. Each clip illustrates the word being said.

**A-roll constants**
- Background
  - Warm beige wall, about #CDB9A3 at the left shading to #B89F86.
  - Dark wood shelf unit on the right, with figurines and red toy cars on the shelves.
  - Sunflower vase and a Pokéball at the left, on an orange-red ledge.
- Subject
  - Photo of a woman centered at about x 48%, with her head at y 28–33%.
  - Red hair, white oversized graphic tee ("PARADISE Resort" print), black trousers.
  - She sits cross-legged on a white chair, with a yellow plush cushion and an orange phone on her lap.
  - She holds a red toy F1 car to her mouth like a mic.
  - Her body width is about 85% of the frame.
- Caption
  - Thin/regular lowercase geometric sans, white #FFFFFF, no stroke, very soft shadow.
  - Cap height about 30–34 px, centered at x 50%, y 14.5–15% (about 280 px from the top).
  - It is one line of 1–3 words.
  - It swaps by hard cut with no entry or exit animation, changing every 0.25–1 s in sync with speech.
- Camera
  - Slow handheld digital punch-in and drift, scale 100–108%.
  - Small jump-zooms are applied on cuts, then ease back out.
