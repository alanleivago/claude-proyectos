**Global style (applies to every shot)**
- Canvas is 1080x1920, 24 fps. B-roll clips are full-bleed 9:16 crops, hard-cut and cut to the voice.
- Talking-head captions use a thin lowercase geometric sans (Outfit/Poppins Light feel).
  - White, centered at x=50%, y≈14.5%, font-size ≈46px (ascender height ≈36px).
  - 2–3 words per chunk. Chunks swap on the beat with no slide, no scale and at most a 1-frame fade.
  - They sit over the soft beige background with no shadow, box or tab.
- Talking-head shots use a slow digital punch-in of about 100→106% over the shot.
- Several transitions end with a 2–3 frame gaussian blur, and then the next shot starts sharp.
