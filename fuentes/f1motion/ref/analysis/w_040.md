**Global notes**
- Frames are 384×682, so multiply by 2.8 for 1080×1920.
- The window alternates between three shot types: screenshot cutaways, talking head with captions, and white-background pixel-art explainers.
- Cut pattern, with frame counts at 24 fps:
  - Screenshot, 22 frames, then constructors screenshot, 36 frames.
  - Talking head, 64 frames, then pixel art, 60 frames.
  - Talking head, 65 frames, then pixel art, 34 frames.
  - Talking head, 52 frames, then pixel art, 100 frames.
  - Talking head, 46+ frames.
- Pixel shots and talking heads are joined by a blur dissolve. The first sampled frame of almost every new shot is heavily Gaussian-blurred (about 20 px), and it sharpens within about 3–4 frames. This is a defocus or zoom-blur transition of about 6 frames.
