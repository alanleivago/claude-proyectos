# Shot list (1080×1920, 24 fps; frame-px ≈ 2.81 × the 384-px sample)

**Global style**
- Captions are white, thin-to-regular lowercase geometric sans, centered at y≈14.5% (cap height ≈30 px). They show as 2–3-word chunks that hard-swap every ~0.5 s with no animation, and they are used only on the talking-head shots.
- Infographic shots are white backgrounds with flat vector or cut-out collage.
- Face cutouts are grayscale photo faces pasted onto vector bodies or vehicles.
- Cuts are mostly hard cuts, with blur or zoom-settle "bookends" on the talking-head shots.

---

### 1. [0.00–1.12] Talking head, "If all you know about…"
- **Background:** warm beige wall (#D9C7B0). Shelf with figurines on the right, sunflowers on the left.
- **Subject:** red-haired woman, centered (x≈50%, face y≈33%, torso ≈75% wide). She holds a toy red F1 car at her mouth like a mic and sits cross-legged with a yellow plush and a phone on her lap.
- **Captions:** "If all you" (0–0.7), then "know about" (0.75–1.1). White, y≈14.5%, centered.
- **Camera:** slow digital push-in of about 5–8% over 1 s, with slight handheld drift.
- **Out:** defocus blur ramps in at ~1.0 s, 3–4 frames, then a hard cut. The blur acts as a "rack-focus" transition.

### 4. [3.71–5.71] "Lewis Hamilton" car
