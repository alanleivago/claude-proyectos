# Shot list, 60.0–80.0 s (1080×1920, 24 fps)

Percentages are of frame width/height. Frames are sampled at 4 fps, so durations are estimates (±2 frames).

## Global style notes for this window
- **Presenter shots:** a single caption line sits at x=50%, y≈14.5%, centered.
  - Font: medium-weight geometric sans (Poppins/Gilroy-like), mixed case.
  - Size: about 42 px font size, cap height about 30 px.
  - Color: white #FFFFFF, no stroke, a faint soft shadow at most.
  - Words are chunked 1–3 per caption. Each chunk is a hard swap with no animation, changing about every 0.25–0.5 s.
- **Reaction clips:** black-and-white with a fine vertical scanline/halftone overlay (about 2 px lines at 40–50% opacity). There are no captions.
- **Cut rhythm:** almost every transition is a hard cut. The exceptions are the blur-ins at 74.75, 76.75 and 79.5, the carousel swipes, and the sticker peels.
