## Common formula
- Male deep VO narration throughout (constant), music bed under it, loud master (-16 LUFS, LRA ~5).
- Burned-in text = the narration itself, phrase by phrase, small (serif in Moon/Sound, monospace in Footprints), placed in empty negative space of the painting, accumulates line-by-line, punch line jumps to another corner. No word-by-word karaoke.
- Art: flat digital paintings with swirling "Van Gogh" wave textures, limited palettes per scene (blue/violet base, then red or amber/green for the turn). ~12–20 paintings per video, held 8–20 s each with slow drift/rotation/push-in and animated swirl; glitch/flicker cut bursts at the reveal (Moon 1:48–1:56: cuts every ~0.13 s).
- Ending: "Maybe the question isn't X. Maybe it's Y." + URBN logo on black, ~7 s silence tail.
- Structure: impossible fact (1 line) -> official/scientific confirmation -> "the strangest part wasn't..." -> investigation (archives, footage, history across civilizations) -> cover-up (sealed, never released) -> horror applied to the viewer/humanity -> question ending.
- Entity never shown. Horror is an absence/evidence (footprints, a missing moon, a breathing sound) plus human reaction faces.

### Patterns
- Premise type that wins: a COLOSSAL entity revealed in scale against something human and sacred (church, worshippers) = top 2 of the shorts with religion (#4, #10) and 3 of the top 7 are religion-framed. Second: gaze reversal ("it looked back", "it smiled at our telescopes", "the Moon blinked") = the biggest long and #6. A familiar giant object doing a HUMAN GESTURE (blink, smile) is the strongest single trigger (728k).
- Weak: body-horror/feeding, children, ocean-floor evidence without a face (all ~10k flat).
- Title formula: (a) aphorism "Some [sacred/natural thing] aren't [X], they're [Y]." (#4, #5, #10, nourishment); (b) "One night, the [huge familiar thing] [human gesture]." (long #1); (c) "It's possible / Maybe we've ..." reframing the viewer. The title is the premise; no question marks in the winners except the black-hole one (9.8k).
- Hook (card 1): calm authority + place: "The small church at the edge of town...", "Scientists found...", "Divers reached...". Never starts with the monster.
- Card 2: the reveal of scale or an impossible detail (legs, finger, breathing, human teeth).
- Ending: one short line that gives the entity INTENT toward us ("Then it smiled at our telescopes.", "until it started to rest over the church itself", "Someone had just left.", "None of them described a known god."). No explanation, no logo in shorts.

### 6 original premises for Madfable (ranked)
1. **"Some bells aren't rung to call the faithful. They're rung to keep something asleep."** Hook: "For 600 years, the monastery bell in the Carpathians rang every hour. Last winter, the last monk died." Entity: a colossal sleeping face in the fog bank above the monastery, eyes opening one hour at a time. Why: top-performing frame (worship + old god, 32.8k) plus a face that looks back (16.5k); clean 3-card short + long structure (hours as chapters).
2. **"Some mountains aren't formed. They're kneeling."** Hook: "Every village in the valley prays toward the same peak. No one remembers why." (Andes / Tibet). Entity: the snowmelt reveals a stone face bowed toward the village; this spring it lifted its head. Why: colossus vs worshippers (#4, #10) and a human gesture by a landscape (Moon blinked).
3. **"One night, the Moon's eclipse had a profile."** Hook: "During the total eclipse, the Earth's shadow on the Moon wasn't round." Entity: the shadow is a human face in profile that slowly turns to look at us. Why: the 728k long formula (familiar giant + human gesture) and the Moon short; strongest long-form candidate, but closest to Urbn's own hit, so make the face carry it.
4. **"Something in the deep is learning our faces."** Hook: "Every dive into the Philippine Trench, the helmet cameras caught a pale face in the dark." Entity: a face that imitates the divers, more accurate each dive; last tape it wears the cameraman's face, wrong. Why: "couldn't get the human form correct" + "watching for a long time" + boundaries (19k); pure Junji face progression.
5. **"Some statues aren't facing the sea. They're turned away from it."** Hook: "Archaeologists dug the island statues out to their waists. Every one had its hands over its eyes." Entity: hundreds of stone faces hiding their eyes, and the one that doesn't. Why: religion-as-discovery (#10) + scale + a face reveal; strong thumbnail.
6. **"If the ocean floor could look back..."** Hook: "The first full sonar map of the Atlantic floor came back with two craters, perfectly paired." Entity: the seabed relief is a face; the next scan, the eyes are open. Why: gaze reversal (16.5k) + deep (most common topic) but ocean-floor shorts plateau at ~10k and it overlaps the Pacific-door idea.

Prior ideas vs the data: none is strongly favoured. "Knocking under ice" fits the "hidden entity below" tier (~10–12k); "The Lost Second" and mirrors have no Urbn equivalent; "This Man" dream face fits the gaze-reversal pattern best of the old ones.
