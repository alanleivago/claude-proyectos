# Madfable thumbnail rules (from "Ref 1 bit pixelated junji ito madfable/ref miniaturas")

- **Frame:** every thumbnail gets `borde encima.jpeg` on top. It is a hand-drawn white rounded frame. Key out the green; outside the frame is black, inside is the picture. Keyed copy: `pieces/lost-second/thumbs/border_1280.png`, made by `pieces/lost-second/tools/border_key.py`.
- **Subject:**
  - one or two characters, big, from the chest up, with expressive eyes;
  - they fill most of the height, with the thin white outline around them;
  - a 4-panel line-up of faces is also used.
- **Colour:**
  - 1-bit black and white with dither and greys;
  - or a red-and-black background with the character kept in black and white;
  - fine film grain over the picture.
- **Main title:** 1 to 3 words, huge (50–80% of the width), white. Two styles:
  - a condensed heavy sans (Impact-like), pixelated, all caps, with a soft pixel-blurred white glow and a dark halo. It sits low-centre and overlaps the subject ("OBSESSION").
  - the channel's pixel serif (That That New Pixel) with a thin black edge. It sits at the top, or across the lower third, in lower case or caps ("5 hours", "JUNJI ITO STYLE").
- **Second line, in red (about RGB 232, 28, 44):** one only, small to medium. Either Japanese in a pixel font with the red spiral logo next to it ("執着" + spiral), or a bold condensed caps line under the title ("FEARS TO FATHOM").
- **Extras:**
  - quoted words in small white pixel caps on black boxes, repeated around the subject ("NO" "NO" "nO" "No");
  - no arrows, circles or emoji.
- **Readability:** every word must still read at 320×180.
