# Niche and persona proposal for our girl

Based on [analisis-A](analisis-A.md) and [analisis-B](analisis-B.md): 7 accounts from the same "studio", all born between Oct 13 and 24, 0 → 23-49k followers in days. None uses a robot / AI angle → open space, and our girl already looks like a robot (Megaman Legends armour, chest orb).

## Formula they all share (what has to stay)
1. Fixed role for the avatar + fixed name for the viewer ("Chairman", "Rookie", "doctor", "lab partner").
2. Fixed opening and closing lines.
3. Chain of "failure → fix", each step labelled on screen: "This is a [term]".
4. Viewer comment read at 35-40% (in sinaboto's top 3).
5. Ending that calls back to the hook, or a "That's why [everyday thing]".
6. ~70 s, ~240 words, ~2.7 words/s, 15-23 shots/min, one-word pixel captions.
7. Hooks that win: an absurdly primitive version ("If you…, you've got a…"), a paradox ("a tooth cures blindness"), an accusation of the viewer ("Your lipstick is made of bugs"), "try this now". Pure history hooks lose.

## Recommendation: "The Digger" — robot archaeologist of human things
- Lore: she is an android from the distant future (the Megaman Legends world is about diggers in ruins). Humans are gone; she digs up "ancient artifacts" (fridge, toilet, microwave, money, toothbrush…) and rebuilds them from scratch to understand how they work.
- Name for the viewer: "Operator" (her mission control).
- Fixed opening: "Operator, I dug up another human artifact…" / Fixed closing: "Artifact understood. Logging off."
- Why it works: it keeps sinaboto's "how X works / build it from zero" structure (the strongest niche: 1-6M) but with a persona nobody else has, robot humour ("humans used to put their butts on WATER?"), robot expressions (dizzy, shocked, hearts) and natural recurring gags (her processor overheating = steam/stink-lines FX).
- Production: ruin sets + MML2 cars/objects as "relics" reuse very well.

## Alternative B: "Unit-07 learns to be human"
A robot studying the human body and everyday habits (why we yawn, why onions make you cry). Like yuimasensei/yuki.science; more science, less building. Lower ceiling according to the data.

## Script template (70 s)
1. 0-3 s Hook (absurd primitive / paradox / accusation) + fixed line.
2. 3-25 s Primitive version → problem → first fix ("This is a…").
3. 25-28 s Viewer comment ("Operator Mike asks…") → twist.
4. 28-60 s 2-4 more fixes, escalating, each with a label and an FX.
5. 60-70 s Payoff "That's why your [thing]…" + callback to the hook + closing line.
