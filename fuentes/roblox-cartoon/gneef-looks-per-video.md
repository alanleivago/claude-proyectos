# What makes these looks "wild"
1. **Silhouette exaggeration:** the hair is the loudest object on every character. The orange hero hair is nearly 1.3× head width with razor-sharp triangles, and the frog hood adds its own bulky silhouette.
2. **Colour clash and palette blocks:** each character is built on one dominant hue, so they read at thumbnail size. Examples are mono-orange for the hero, mono-green for the frog and Speedy, and black plus purple for the villain.
3. **Scale gags:** the giant yellow hammer is about 1.5× the avatar. The crown and the red mane are oversized compared with the head.
4. **Meme items:** the classic noob, the frog hat, the hot dog, the lemonade stand, the ban-hammer admin tool, and the "STOP FOR CHILDREN" creepy van.
5. **Face contrast:** the faces are minimalist black line decals on flat colour. Expressions swing between extreme states: shock "o", teeth grin, tongue laugh, "^ ^" squint, heavy-brow rage.
6. **Texture contrast:** soft cable-knit and wool sweater textures sit against flat-shaded plastic hair and glowing particle FX.

# Quick three.js build notes
- **Hair:** one mesh per hair style with flat shading (no smooth normals) and a slightly lighter material for the highlight strands.
- **Faces:** decal textures on the head front face, with a 2px black stroke, a transparent background, and a separate blush overlay.
- **Shirt/pants:** tileable knit textures plus colour-blocked cuffs.
- **Glow:** additive sprites for the sparkles, plus a bloom pass on the hammer and gun.

# What makes these looks "wild"

4. **Scale exaggeration.** Oversized boxy sweater sleeves, a giant pink fluffy mass on the driver, a big chest emblem on the hero, and a head slightly too big for the body on the protagonist.

# What makes the looks "wild"
- **Scale breaks:** The doll's head fills the frame like a giant. Marionettes are copy-pasted by the dozen, with 20+ extras on a lit platform. Shelves of music boxes recede into the dark.
- **Colour clash:** Pastel lavender and pink pyjamas sit against pitch-black night streets and deep crimson/purple factory lighting. The magenta shirt against a flat saturated blue background is deliberately jarring.
- **Texture contrast:** Chunky knit textures on sweaters, sheets and blankets sit beside glossy porcelain skin and rusty metal. The tactile mix is cozy and creepy at once.
- **Meme and costume items:** The dino hood with teeth, the green nightcap with diamond studs, and the rainbow item on the bed.
- **Horror cues from simple parts:** Hollow eye sockets, a cracked skull, a wind-up key in the back, bare skeletal hands, and a hem stained with rust or dirt.
- **Exaggerated faces on stock bodies:** Rage faces with heavy lines on a default blocky avatar, and a static blank doll face. Few shapes carry a lot of emotion.
- **Uniform crowd with varied hair:** Matching pastel pyjamas make the crowd look like a set of toys, while the hair (bun, curls, ponytail) gives each one an identity.

**For your three.js kit:** The biggest needs are a separate "doll" rig (long neck, thin limbs, ruffled collar mesh, key mesh), a knit-texture normal or bump map shared across sweaters and bedding, a glossy low-roughness material for the porcelain, and string-line geometry for the marionettes.

Notes: the frames are cropped and lit differently, so a few details (like hero skin tone) shift between shots. I flag those below. Proportions are standard Roblox blocky everywhere. The main deviation is that **legs are replaced by a single tapered mermaid-tail mesh with a fin**.

---

## 1. HERO, orange-haired mermaid kid

**Role:** protagonist, panicked about holding his breath, then captured and dunked in a bucket.

**Hair**
- Colour: saturated orange (~#E8641B), with a darker orange shadow layer underneath.
- Shape: messy, layered, spiky. Chunky shards pointing up and out at the crown, two or three tufts flicking sideways at the temples, and a side-swept fringe that breaks into 3–4 points over the forehead.
- Volume: about 1.3× head width, sitting directly on the scalp with no gap. Build it as a single mesh of stacked wedge or cone clusters.
- No hat.

**Outfit**
- Top: oversized cable-knit sweater, cream/beige with 3 wide horizontal tan bands. The knit texture is a vertical braided pattern.
- Cuffs and hem: a thick orange rib band at each sleeve end, in the same orange as the hair. The neckline is a round, slightly raised collar.
- Sleeves are boxy and wider than the default arm.
- Bottom: single mermaid tail. In the day shots it is beige/tan, and in the bedroom it reads pale lavender-white (probably lighting). The fin is a flat two-lobed shape.
- Accessory: round white wrist timer with a black screen and mint-green digits, showing "2:40" (frame 10).

**Skin:** orange/tangerine (~#F08A5D), pinker in the bedroom frame. Default blocky body, with the head slightly large relative to the torso.

**Faces**
| Moment | Drawing spec |
|---|---|
| Holding breath ("breathe") | Eyes are half-lidded black ovals with a heavy upper-lid line. Brows are thin and tilted up toward the middle (worried). Tiny teardrop or tear marks sit under the eyes. The mouth is a wide, thick-line "U" smile, a forced grin. Sweat droplets and bubbles float around the head as separate sprites. |
| Bedroom shrug | Round black dot eyes with tiny highlights. Brows are slanted worried. The mouth is a small open "o". |
| Lying on floor, timer | Round black eyes, brows tilted up-inward (sad), and a thin downturned frown arc. |
| Underwater, smug | A sly half-smile and one brow lowered, with small bubble dots and sweat on the face. |

Line weight is medium-thin for brows and mouth, and the eyes are solid fills.

## 2. YELLOW NOOB, sidekick / comic relief (captured with the hero)

- **Hair:** golden yellow, a short swoopy fringe with one curled lock at the front and a small flick at the top. It is low-volume and hugs the skull.
- **Skin:** classic Roblox yellow (#F5CD30) on head, hands and arms.
- **Outfit:** plain white blocky torso (flat white shirt, no graphic). The lower body is a white tail or legs, and he wears the same white sleeve cuff texture as the hero in one frame.
- **Faces:**
  - **Default smug:** two black oval eyes, one brow raised higher (asymmetrical), and a small red crescent smile with no teeth.
  - **Bucket grin:** a huge white open grin with teeth, big eyes and one brow arched.
  - **Panic underwater:** huge white round eyes with tiny black pupils, wide mouth showing teeth, and bubbles around him.
- He appears holding or tethered to a pink worm and hook in the "free" frame.

## What makes the looks "wild"

1. **Scale exaggeration:** the afro is nearly 2× head size, the seahorses are rideable giants, and the sweater sleeves are boxy and oversized.
2. **Single-hue characters:** the hero's orange hair, skin, cuffs and rug all match, giving a monochrome-orange silhouette against cyan water. The lifeguard is almost entirely red, and the noob is entirely yellow and white.
3. **Colour clash:** saturated orange against teal and cyan, red coral against green grass, and magenta worm against brown wood. High-contrast complementary pairs are used everywhere.
4. **Meme items:** the "SURFACE PATROL" lifeguard in mermaid land, a "BEWARE OF SHARK" sign in a suburban yard, a fisherman with a worm on a hook as bait, air-bubble helmets, and a literal countdown timer on the wrist.
5. **Face-decal comedy:** sweat drops, tears, bubbles and one-brow-raised smugs are layered on top of simple dot eyes. The fisherman's red ring mouth and line-across-face is deliberately uncanny.
6. **Silhouette variety:** spiky orange, flat white cap, floppy olive hat, huge afro, buns and a topknot. Every character can be identified from the outline alone.

## Implementation tips for your three.js kit
- Make a **tail mesh** (tapered box or lathe plus a flat two-lobed fin) that replaces the legs.
- Build **hair as separate meshes** (spiky cones for the hero, a sphere for the afro, a cap plus braid for the captain).
- Draw **face decals** on a 256×256 canvas with swappable expressions: eye shape, brow angle, mouth type, and optional sweat/tear/blush overlays.
- Add **bubble helmets** using `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with transmission.
- Use a **cuff texture strip** on the sleeves for the orange rib detail.
