**1. Hair (biggest gap)**
- **Silhouette:** Ours is a rounded dome, about 10-15% wider and taller at the crown, with a boxy, vertical-sided back. His is tighter at the temples and tapers to a wedge. Cut crown height by about 10%, pull the temples in, and taper the back toward the nape.
- **V bangs:** Our centre spike is too short and thin. It should reach the nose bridge, about 1.5× wider at its root. The gaps between spikes are tiny, and the side spikes barely read. Open two clear triangular gaps, and make the side spikes about 2× longer and sharper.
- **Side locks:** Ours are fat, flat slabs with blunt tips that hide the ear zone. His are narrower, angular shards that sweep forward. Narrow them by about 30% and add a sharper point at the jaw.
- **Nape flick:** Ours is a small zigzag hem. His flicks out and back noticeably. Extend it by about 2× and angle it outward.
- **Facets and colour:** Ours has fine vertical streaks and a uniform saturated purple. His has bigger flat facets and a dark-root-to-bright gradient. Use fewer, larger facets and add the gradient.

**2. Head volume and profile**
- The front reads fine, but the sides and back look hollow and flat. At h_60 to h_120 and h_240 to h_300 there is a large flat beige wedge between jaw, hair and neck.
- The skull behind the face is boxy, and the jaw has no depth.
- The profile is weak. The forehead and brow are hidden or flat, the lips are small, and the chin is soft and slightly receding.
- The neck is a thick column. His neck is longer and more slender, with a more pointed chin.

**3. Ears**
- They are effectively missing. Round ears are not visible at most angles because the locks cover them. At h_60/h_90/h_240/h_270 the ear area is blank skin.
- Only a tiny ear shows around h_120. Add visible round ears at roughly eye-to-nose height, and tuck the locks in front of them.

**4. Face texture**
- The realistic photo clashes with the low-poly PS1 look of the body and hair.
- The face is pale and pinkish, while the neck and side planes are tan and yellow. There is a visible tone seam at the jaw and neck.
- Cheek texture stretches toward the profile, and the side planes have no texture detail.
- Colour-match the skin across the face, neck and ears, and consider downsampling or posterising the face.

**5. Geometry defects**
- There are no true holes.
- Hair flaps look detached. At h_30 a dark lock floats behind the ear. At h_120 to h_150 a skin gap shows between the lock and the neck. The back hair reads as a separate box from the skull.
- Nothing visibly pokes through, but the hair should sit closer to the scalp.

**Scores:** Hair 5/10. Head 4.5/10.

**Top 5 fixes**
1. Resculpt the bangs: a longer, wider centre spike, open gaps and sharp side spikes.
2. Slim the crown and back from a dome to a tapered wedge, and narrow the side locks into angular shards.
3. Add visible round ears and fill the flat side and back skin areas with real skull and jaw volume.
4. Match the skin tones and fix the seams between face, neck and ears.
5. Enlarge the nape flick, use bigger facets with a root gradient, and close the gaps between the hair and the head.
