**1. Hair (6/10)**
- **Silhouette:** Ours reads as a rounded helmet or bucket, wider at the temples and flatter on top. The reference is a tighter, taller, more angular bob. Raise the crown about 8–10% and pull the temple and side volume in about 10%.
- **Bangs:** The centre spike is too short and blunt. Lengthen it to the nose bridge, about 40% longer, and make it narrower and sharper. The side spikes are almost absent. Add two distinct, pointed spikes aimed at the cheekbones. Widen the forehead gaps between the spikes so skin shows, as in the reference.
- **Side locks:** They run to the collar and are too thick, and the lighter lavender tip gradient is wrong. Shorten them to chin or jaw level, about 30% shorter. Narrow them about 25%, give them a pointed tip, and keep them straight and close to the cheek.
- **Nape flick:** Ours is a notched hem with a weak sideways kick. The reference flicks outward and back in a clear wedge. Extend the flick about 15% and make it a single bold spike.
- **Facets and colour:** The strand texture is too soft and streaky. Use fewer, larger flat facets with hard value steps. The purple is close, but the gradient makes it too light and glossy.

**2. Head volume and profile (5/10)**
- **Profile:** At h_90 and h_270 the face looks like a projected mask. The forehead and brow are flat, the nose barely protrudes, the lips are shallow and the chin is weak. Push the nose out about 10–15% and add a brow ridge and a defined chin.
- **Skull:** From h_150 to h_210 the back of the skull is a narrow, boxy slab. Add depth behind the ears.
- **Neck:** It looks thin and tan.
- **Overall:** It holds up from the front but flattens from the side.

**3. Ears**
They are visible only from h_90 to h_120 and h_240 to h_270, where they read as dark painted shapes. They look like dents rather than protruding round ears, so they need real geometry and a lighter skin tone. They are hidden by the locks from the front and 3/4. The reference ear is clearly visible, so open a little more hair clearance around ours.

**4. Face texture**
- The face skin is pale and pink, while the neck, ear and back of the head are darker brown. This creates a visible tone break at the jaw and ear line at 3/4 and profile.
- There is stretching on the cheek and jaw edge in profile.
- The chest texture is pixelated.
- Blend or colour-match the face to the neck and head skin.

**5. Geometry defects**
- There are dark detached-looking hair flaps behind the ear at h_30, h_60 and h_300. They are probably back-facing or shadowed polygons.
- Thin lock tips poke out past the neck at h_150 to h_210.
- I see no holes.

**Scores:** Hair 6/10, head 5/10.

**Top 5 fixes**
1. Rebuild the bangs with a long centre spike, sharp side spikes and open forehead gaps.
2. Shorten and narrow the side locks, and remove the light tip gradient.
3. Deepen the profile with a stronger nose, brow, chin and back-skull volume.
4. Colour-match the skin across the face, ear and neck seams, and build real round ear geometry.
5. Sharpen the nape flick, use bolder facets, and fix the dark flap polygons.
